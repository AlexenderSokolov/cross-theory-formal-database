\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Let $\Omega\subset\R^3$ be a bounded domain of class $C^{2,1}$ (i.e., the derivative of the curvatures is bounded). The singular values of $H_m$ can be estimated as follows:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]\item $\lim_{m\to+\infty}\lambda_{k,m}=\lambda_k$, for all $k\in\N^*$.
\end{enumerate}\end{theorem}
