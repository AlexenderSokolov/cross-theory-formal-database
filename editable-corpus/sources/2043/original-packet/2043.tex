The author builds a lattice-prediction scheme, counts its exceptional choices by an entropy estimate, and combines the unstable-projection cover with the complementary Oseledets directions. The corrected order of choosing the parameters is essential to that dimension bound, and the entire authored covering construction remains in the packet.
