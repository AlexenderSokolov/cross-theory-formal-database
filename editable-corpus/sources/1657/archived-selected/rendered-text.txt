Selected task scope: Exactly Theorem4.18: N>=3; the complex type-A aﬀine Yangian has parameters
epsilon_1,epsilon_2 and hbar=epsilon_1+epsilon_2; the target aﬀine-gl_N central element is special-
ized to the scalar c with hbar*c=N*epsilon_2 and epsilon_2!=0. For every integer m the explicitly
displayed a_m, using the finite R_m of Proposition4.16, satisfies ev(a_m)=1(m); hence the image in
the specified completed enveloping target contains the ordinary enveloping algebra of aﬀine gl_N at
this central specialization. This is an image/preimage assertion. It does not assert that the a_m sat-
isfy Heisenberg commutation relations inside the aﬀine Yangian, nor count the irreducibility corollary
separately.
Diﬀiculty: H2: A diagonal loop current cannot be recovered by simply applying the existing aﬀine-
sl evaluation map. The author constructs braid-translated degree-one commutators, tracks the aﬀine
central terms, and proves that their infinite completed quadratic corrections cancel to a finite element of
the aﬀine-sl enveloping subalgebra. The resulting explicit preimages for all positive, zero and negative
modes establish a substantial quantum-algebra image theorem.
