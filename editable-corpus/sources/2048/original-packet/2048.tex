\paragraph{Editorial problem boundary.}
Prove only Theorem 1.6 below on a smooth bounded connected Euclidean domain, with fixed real bounded potential, Dirichlet parabolic transport and the constructed fixed internal observation set. The exact smooth family of potentials gives an arbitrarily large separation of uniform viscous observation time from the uniformly bounded flushing time. Full field construction, quasimode existence, weighted eigenfunction estimates and exit-time argument are retained. Boundary observations, positive-solution equality and arbitrary fixed-vector-field metric claims are context only.
\paragraph{Difficulty (editorial): H2.}
The geometric flushing condition does not itself control the small-viscosity observability time. The author constructs a smooth family with a fixed positive bottom energy, combines the weighted eigenfunction/quasimode estimates with its observation geometry, and proves a uniformly bounded exit time for the corresponding trajectories. The same family then has a growing observation lower bound, so the separation requires both the spectral construction and the explicit dynamical argument.
