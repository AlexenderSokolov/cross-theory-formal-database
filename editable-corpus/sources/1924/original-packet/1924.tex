\paragraph{Editorial problem boundary.}
Prove only Theorem 6.4.2 below in the unramified case, with \(X(K^{\mathrm{ur}})\ne\varnothing\) to avoid the empty-boundary normalisation convention. Here \(K\) is a non-archimedean local field, \(X\) is smooth and proper of pure dimension, and the nonzero pluricanonical form satisfies the printed componentwise condition. The normalised measures are exactly those in Definition 6.4.1, and finite unramified extensions are ordered by inclusion. The selected weak limit is the weighted Shilov-atom measure of the original theorem. Definitions, model-invariance proof, point-separating functions, analytic measure charts, residue-tube estimate and complete Lang--Weil proof are retained. Ramified/skeleton-limit assertions are context only.
\paragraph{Difficulty (editorial): H2.}
Weak Néron smoothening closes arbitrary vertical model-function tests, while étale analytic residue tubes convert pluricanonical integrals to finite-field point counts. Lang-Weil isolates minimal canonical-order components, yielding an intrinsic Berkovich atomic limit without resolution or semistable reduction.
