of simple vertex operator algebras. Let us denote these vertex operator algebras as LW (2,2) (cL , cW )
and LH (cL , cL,I ). It was proved in [4] that simple vertex operator algebra LW (2,2) (cL , cW ) embeds

4 Screening operators and W (2, 2)-algebra
We think that the vertex algebra LW (2,2) (cL , cW ) is a very interesting example of non-rational vertex
algebra, which admits similar fusion ring of representations as some W-algebras appearing in LCFT (cf.
