Selected task scope: Exactly Theorem5: integers g>L>=0, n>=1, nonnegative ordered d_i with sum
d_i=3g-3+n and, for n>=3, sum of first n-2 d_i<=L; n=1,2 use the explicit unrestricted Pi_L conven-
tions. The intersection number is at least lambda(g,L)*(6g-5+2n)!!/(product_i(2d_i+1)!!*g!*24^g),
where lambda(g,L)=product_{i=0}^{L-1}(1-1/(6(g-i)+1))*(1-2/(6(g-L)-1)); the empty product for
L=0 is specified. The result is a lower bound, not a two-sided uniform asymptotic theorem.
Diﬀiculty: H2: The central obligation is to maintain an explicit multiplicative error bound uniformly
in the number and distribution of descendant exponents. The proof builds compatible defects for the
string, dilaton and Virasoro recursions and uses a coupled induction on the small-exponent sum and
the number of zero entries, with separate genus-lowering terms. This is a substantive moduli-space
intersection-number estimate, beyond evaluating a few standard correlators or applying the recursion
mechanically.
