    Since basic hypergeometric series (1.1) is a q-analog of hypergeometric series (1.2), whether there
exists a q-analog of Theorem 1.1 is a natural question. To answer this question, we use the theory of
q-Borel summability. It is a q-analog of the theory of Borel summability and studied by many authors
(cf. Ramis [10], Zhang [13, 14], Di Vizio–Zhang [3] and Dreyfus–Eloy [6]). Dreyfus [4] proved that
for every formal power series solution of a linear q-difference equation with rational coeﬀicients, we
may construct a meromorphic solution of the same equation applying several q-Borel and q-Laplace
transformations of appropriate orders and appropriate direction.
