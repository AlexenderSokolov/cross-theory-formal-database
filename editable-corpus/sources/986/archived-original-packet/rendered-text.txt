Proposition 4.1. Every consistent Markov semigroup is conservative.

Proof. Let (Pt )t≥0 be Markov semigroup on (N<∞ , N<∞ ) that is consistent, i.e., Pt Af = APt f for
all t ≥ 0 and all measurable f : N<∞ → R+ . Then, for all k ∈ N,
