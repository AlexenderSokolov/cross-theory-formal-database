\begin{thm} \label{thm:allfactors}
For every real number $\lambda>2$, there exists a substitution $\varrho$ on an (infinite) compact alphabet $\mathcal{A}$ such that the inflation factor of the associated inflation rule is $\lambda$ and every element of the subshift $(X_{\varrho},\sigma)$ gives rise to a Delone set. 
\end{thm}
