1 Introduction and summary
This paper is in memory of Richard Paris. He was an expert in special functions and asymptotics,
especially the use of Mellin–Barnes integral representations. Most of the techniques that we use in
this paper are all coming from his book [12] (co-authored by David Kaminski). Note that in [11]
Richard discusses the large a asymptotics for the Lerch transcendent, and he uses a Mellin–Barnes
integral representation that is with respect to a, whereas (1.5) is with respect to z.

what they do in [13], but somehow they do miss the fact that there will also be contributions from
the branch-point at t = −a.
