\section*{Editable transcription of original figure labels}
\noindent\textbf{Source-figure transcription, separate from native author TeX.} The following is a faithful transcription of printed labels in the unchanged original panels. Panel names and geometric/arrow locators are editorial locating aids only; the displayed matrix labels are author figure text, not new equations or derived entries. Figures retain their original curves and arrows. Signs, hats, inverses, printed cases and indices have not been inferred or repaired. In particular the zero-parametrix panel \texttt{5b2.pdf} prints subscript $\mathbf{E}_0$, whereas some other panels print superscript $\mathbf{E}^{0}$; this literal distinction is preserved. Source asset hashes and exact locators are in \texttt{figure-label-transcription-map.json}.
