\paragraph{Editorial problem boundary.}
Prove only Theorem B below with \(n\ge2\), \(\tau>n-1\), \(l>2(\tau+1)\), the printed frequency domains, and its sufficiently small \(C^l\) perturbation. The invariant Lagrangian graphs and frequency parameter map have the stated regularity and Lipschitz estimates. Full analytic step, iteration and convergence estimates are retained. No Whitney differentiability, frequency-Lipschitz dynamics or separate measure-corollary claim is selected.
\paragraph{Difficulty (editorial): H3.}
Finite differentiability analytic smoothing, an adapted KAM iteration and sharper graph-composition estimates establish frequency-Lipschitz invariant graphs without frequency-Lipschitz conjugacies.
