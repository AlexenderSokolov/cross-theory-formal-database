In this paper we consider second kind integrals on algebraic curves, which are second kind 0-forms

the first non-vanishing derivative of sigma function on the Abel’s image of ξ. The first non-vanishing
derivative of sigma function has Sato weight equal to −g, the corresponding differential operator has
Sato weight − wgt σ(u) − g. Recall that Sato weight of sigma function is negative and calculated by the
formula −(n2 − 1)(s2 − 1)/24 for (n, s)-curve, see [2].
