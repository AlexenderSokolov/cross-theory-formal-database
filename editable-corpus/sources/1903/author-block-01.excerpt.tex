\begin{remark*}
As explained in Section~2, this paper is actually written in a more general framework, as we only need $\jepPubScript{I}$ to be an abstract masure and $G$ to be a strongly transitive group of (positive, type-preserving) automorphisms of $\jepPubScript{I}$. In particular, this applies to almost split (and not only split) Kac--Moody groups over local fields.
\end{remark*}
