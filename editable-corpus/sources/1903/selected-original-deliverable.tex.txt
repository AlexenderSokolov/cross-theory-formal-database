\paragraph{Source and attribution.}
Ramla Abdellatif and Auguste Hébert, \emph{Completed Iwahori-Hecke algebras and parahoric Hecke algebras for Kac-Moody groups over local fields}, Journal de l’École polytechnique -- Mathématiques 6 (2019), 79--118, DOI 10.5802/jep.88. \href{https://jep.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/jep.88/}{Exact publisher article}, \href{https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/}{CC BY 4.0}.
\paragraph{Editorial problem boundary.}
Exact original Theorem 3.1 for a type 0 local face whose vertex lies in the cocharacter lattice, its fixer, and infinite vectorial Weyl group. Retained are the necessary introductory Remark, all original Sections 2--3 and the complete 28-entry bibliography. The root-generating system has independent simple roots and simple coroots; the masure is thick of finite thickness with a positive, type-preserving strongly transitive group and the stated affine apartment stabilizer. The entire original 41-page PDF is preserved separately. Unrelated introductory completed-algebra material and all Sections 4--5 are excluded from this selected carrier.
\paragraph{Difficulty (editorial): H2.}
The proof uses masure retractions and the authored half-apartment fixer lemma to identify point, wall and chamber orbits. The three parts of the wall-to-chamber bijection and alternating finite-thickness count give unbounded stabilizer orbits from infinitely many positive real roots. This new local orbit-growth chain is retained in full before the compact-open finite-index contradiction; no helper result or completed-center variant is counted.
\paragraph{Literal source and version disclosure.}
Original Section 2.2 literally prints $(w\cdot x=1)\Rightarrow(w=1)$ next to the simply transitive assertion. Original Lemma 3.3 and the complete retraction uniqueness Remark 3.2 remain as published, including the half-apartment reference and footnote; no extra case argument is inserted. The advertised publisher native file returned HTTP 404. This is an independently typeset, publisher-checked editable transcription, with no native author-byte equality claim. The older nonexclusive arXiv v2 archive was a private, read-only, unexecuted aid; no private class, preamble, comment or source file is loaded or exported.
