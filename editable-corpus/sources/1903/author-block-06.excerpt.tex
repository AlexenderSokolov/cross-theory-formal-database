\subsubsection{Apartments and masure of type \texorpdfstring{$\jepPubBbb{A}$}{A}}
An apartment of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$ is a set $A$ together with a non-empty set $\operatorname{Isom}(\jepPubBbb{A},A)$ of bijections (called Weyl-isomorphisms) such that, given $f_0\in\operatorname{Isom}(\jepPubBbb{A},A)$, the elements of $\operatorname{Isom}(\jepPubBbb{A},A)$ are exactly the bijections of the form $f_0\circ w$ with $w\in W$. All the isomorphisms considered in the sequel will be Weyl-isomorphisms, hence we will only write isomorphism instead of Weyl-isomorphism.

An isomorphism $\varphi:A\to A'$ between two apartments of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$ is a bijection such that: $f\in\operatorname{Isom}(\jepPubBbb{A},A)\Longleftrightarrow\varphi\circ f\in\operatorname{Isom}(\jepPubBbb{A},A')$. By construction, all the notions that are preserved under the action of $W$ can be extended to any apartment of type $A$. For instance, we can define sectors, enclosures, faces or chimneys in any apartment $A$ of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$, as well as a pre-order $\leqslant_A$ on $A$.

We can now define the most important object of this section: the masures of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$.
\begin{definition}\label{jep88localDefinition2_4}
A masure of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$ is a set $\jepPubScript{I}$ endowed with a covering $\jepPubScript{A}$ of subsets (called apartments) such that the five following axioms hold.
\begin{description}
\item[(MA1)] Any $A\in\jepPubScript{A}$ can actually be endowed with a structure of apartment of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$.
\item[(MA2)] If $F$ is a point (resp. a germ of a preordered interval, a generic ray, a solid chimney) contained in an apartment $A$ and if $A'$ is an other apartment containing $F$, then $A\cap A'$ contains $\operatorname{cl}_A(F)$ and there exists an isomorphism from $A$ to $A'$ that fixes $\operatorname{cl}_A(F)$.
\item[(MA3)] If $\jepPubFrak{R}$ is the germ of a splayed chimney and if $F$ is a face or a germ of a solid chimney, then there exists an apartment that contains both $\jepPubFrak{R}$ and $F$.
\item[(MA4)] If two apartments $A$, $A'$ contain both $\jepPubFrak{R}$ and $F$ as in (MA3), then there exists an isomorphism from $A$ to $A'$ that fixes $\operatorname{cl}_A(\jepPubFrak{R}\cup F)$.
\item[(MAO)] If $x$ and $y$ are two points that are both contained in two apartments $A$ and $A'$ and such that $x\leqslant_A y$, then the two segments $[x,y]_A$ and $[x,y]_{A'}$ are equal.
\end{description}
\end{definition}
Recall that saying that an apartment contains a germ of a filter means that it contains at least one element of this germ. Similarly, a map fixes a germ when it fixes at least one element of this germ.

From now on, $\jepPubScript{I}$ will denote a masure of type $\jepPubBbb{A}$. We assume that $\jepPubScript{I}$ is thick of finite thickness, which means that the number of chambers ($=$ alcoves) containing a given panel is finite and greater or equal to three. We also assume that there exists a group $G$ of automorphisms of $\jepPubScript{I}$ that acts strongly transitively on $\jepPubScript{I}$, which implies that all the isomorphisms involved in the axioms above are induced by the action of elements of $G$. We fix an apartment in $\jepPubScript{I}$ that we identify with $\jepPubBbb{A}$ and call the fundamental apartment of $\jepPubScript{I}$. As the action of $G$ on $\jepPubScript{I}$ is strongly transitive, the apartments of $\jepPubScript{I}$ are exactly the sets $g\cdot\jepPubBbb{A}$ with $g\in G$. Let $N$ be the stabilizer of $\jepPubBbb{A}$ in $G$: it defines a group of affine automorphisms of $\jepPubBbb{A}$, and we assume that this group is $W^v\ltimes Y$. As we will see in Section~2.4, these assumptions are not very restrictive for our purpose as they are all satisfied by the masure attached to a split Kac--Moody group $G$ over a non-Archimedean local field (see also~\cite{jep88refGR08} and~\cite{jep88refRou16}).
\begin{remark}\label{jep88localRemark2_5}
In a recent work~\cite[\S5]{jep88refHeb17a}, the second author gives a much simpler axiomatic for masures. To simplify the arguments, the reader can assume that $G$ is an affine Kac--Moody group, in which case the three axioms (MA2), (MA4) and (MAO) can be replaced by the following statement~\cite[Th.~5.38]{jep88refHeb17a}: for any two apartments $A$ and $A'$ in $\jepPubScript{I}$, we have $A\cap A'=\operatorname{cl}(A\cap A')$ and there exists an isomorphism from $A$ to $A'$ that fixes $A\cap A'$. This partially explains why the affine case is less technical, as it does not require to question the existence of isomorphisms that fix subsets of an intersection of apartments.
\end{remark}
\begin{remark}\label{jep88localRemark2_6}
Let $F$ be a local face of an apartment $A$ and $A'$ be another apartment that contains $F$. Then $F$ is also a local face of $A'$ and there exists an isomorphism from $A$ to $A'$ that fixes $F$. Indeed, if $x$ is the vertex of $F$ and if $J$ is a germ of a preordered segment based at $x$ and contained in $F$, then the enclosure of $J$ contains $F$ and the application of (MA2) to $J$ now proves the claim.
\end{remark}
\begin{remark}\label{jep88localRemark2_7}
Pick $w\in W$ and $\jepPubScript{F}$ a filter on $\jepPubBbb{A}$ fixed by $w$: then $w$ fixes $\operatorname{cl}(\jepPubScript{F})$. Combined with the argument used in Remark~\ref{jep88localRemark2_6}, this proves here that for any vectorial face $F^v$ and any base point $x$, the fixer in $G$ of the face $F(x,F^v)$ is exactly the fixer in $G$ of the corresponding local face $F^\ell(x,F^v)$.
\end{remark}
\begin{remark}\label{jep88localRemark2_8}
As we noticed earlier, each apartment $A$ of $\jepPubScript{I}$ can be endowed with a pre-order $\leqslant_A$ induced by $\leqslant_{\jepPubBbb{A}}$. Let $A$ be an apartment of $\jepPubScript{I}$ and $x,y$ be two points in $A$ such that $x\leqslant_A y$. By~\cite[Prop.~5.4]{jep88refRou11}, we know that for any apartment $A'$ of $\jepPubScript{I}$ that contains both $x$ and $y$, we also have $x\leqslant_{A'}y$. We hence get a relation $\leqslant$ on $\jepPubScript{I}$ and~\cite[Th.~5.9]{jep88refRou11} ensures that this relation is a $G$-invariant pre-order on $\jepPubScript{I}$.
\end{remark}
