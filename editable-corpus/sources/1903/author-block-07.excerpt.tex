\subsection{Masure attached to a split Kac--Moody group}
As in~\cite{jep88refTit87} or in~\cite[Chap.~8]{jep88refRem02}, we consider the group functor $\jepPubBold{G}$ associated with the root generating system $\jepPubScript{S}$ fixed in Section~2.1. This functor goes from the category of rings to the category of groups and satisfies axioms (KMG1)--(KMG9) of~\cite{jep88refTit87}. For any field $R$, the group $\jepPubBold{G}(R)$ is uniquely determined by these axioms~\cite[Th.~1']{jep88refTit87}. Furthermore, this functor $\jepPubBold{G}$ contains a toric functor $\jepPubBold{T}$ (denoted by $\jepPubScript{T}$ in~\cite{jep88refRem02}) that goes from the category of rings to the category of abelian groups, and two functors $\jepPubBold{U}^{\pm}$ going from the category of rings to the category of groups.

In particular, let $\jepPubScript{K}$ be a non-Archimedean local field. Denote by $\jepPubScript{O}$ its ring of integers, by $\varpi$ a fixed uniformizer of $\jepPubScript{O}$, by $q$ the cardinality of the residue class field $\jepPubScript{O}/\varpi\jepPubScript{O}$ and set $G:=\jepPubBold{G}(\jepPubScript{K})$ (as well as $U^\pm:=\jepPubBold{U}^{\pm}(\jepPubScript{K})$, $T:=\jepPubBold{T}(\jepPubScript{K})$, etc.). For any sign $\varepsilon\in\{+,-\}$ and any root $\alpha\in\Phi^\varepsilon$, there is an isomorphism $x_\alpha$ from $\jepPubScript{K}$ to a root group $U_\alpha$. For any integer $k\in\jepPubBbb{Z}$, we get a subgroup $U_{\alpha,k}:=x_\alpha(\varpi^k\jepPubScript{O})$ of $U_\alpha$ (see~\cite[\S3.1]{jep88refGR08} for precise definitions). Let $\jepPubScript{I}$ denote the masure attached to $G$ in~\cite{jep88refRou17}: then the following properties hold.
\begin{itemize}
\item The fixer of $\jepPubBbb{A}$ in $G$ is $H:=\jepPubBold{T}(\jepPubScript{O})$~\cite[Rem.~3.2]{jep88refGR08}.
\item The fixer of $\{0\}$ in $G$ is $K_s:=\jepPubBold{G}(\jepPubScript{O})$~\cite[Exam.~3.14]{jep88refGR08}. Applying (MA2) to $\{0\}$ and using Remark~\ref{jep88localRemark2_7}, we get that $K_s$ is also the fixer in $G$ of the face $F_0$.
\item For any pair $(\alpha,k)\in\Phi\times\jepPubBbb{Z}$, the fixer of $D(\alpha,k)$ in $G$ is $H.U_{\alpha,k}$~\cite[Exer.~4.2.7)]{jep88refGR08}.
\item For any sign $\varepsilon\in\{+,-\}$, $U^\varepsilon$ is the fixer in $G$ of $\varepsilon\infty$~\cite[Exam.~4.2.4)]{jep88refGR08}.
\end{itemize}
Moreover, each panel is contained in $q+1$ chambers, hence $\jepPubScript{I}$ is thick of finite thickness.
\begin{remark}\label{jep88localRemark2_9}
The group $G$ is reductive iff $W^v$ is finite. In this case, $\jepPubScript{I}$ is the usual Bruhat--Tits building of $G$ and we have $\jepPubScript{T}=\jepPubBbb{A}$ and $Y^+=Y$.
\end{remark}
