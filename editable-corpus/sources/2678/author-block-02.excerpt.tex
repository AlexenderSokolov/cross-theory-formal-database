The author is grateful to Misha Rudnev for many helpful discussions, and to the anonymous referee for many helpful comments. Finally, as the author was finishing the paper, it came to his attention that, using related methods, Conlon and Lim \cite{CL2021} have independently obtained an estimate akin to Theorem $\ref{diff}$. They are able to obtain a sharp $O_{d}(1)$ error term, instead of our $O_{d}(|A|^{1- \delta})$ term, for all sufficiently large subsets $A$ of $\mathbb{R}^d$.
