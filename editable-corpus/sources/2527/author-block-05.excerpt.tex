This paper seeks to provide a complete description of normed spaces which are of KKL type.  It is straightforward to see that a normed space  $(X, \|\cdot \|)$ that is of KKL type must also be of \textit{Rademacher type 2} (or just type 2), meaning that there exists a constant $T\in (0,\infty)$ such that for all $x_1,...,x_n \in X$ we have that
\begin{align}\label{type2}
\mathbb{E}\Big\| \sum_{j=1}^{n} \varepsilon_{j} x_{j} \Big\|^{2} \leq T^{2}  \sum_{j=1}^{n} \|x_j\|^2.
\end{align}
The best constant $T$ in (\ref{type2}) is denoted by $T_{2}(X)$.
