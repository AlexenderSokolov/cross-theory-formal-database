Let E = nj=1 Lj and F = ni=1 Mi be sums of line bundles over a smooth projective variety B.
Choose r < n, and define the vector bundle V → B to be the total space of

                              Hom(F, OB ⊗ Cr ) × Hom(OB ⊗ Cr , E).

This vector bundle comes equipped with a (right) action of G = Aut(Cr ) = GLr given fiberwise by
                                                            
                                   (X, Y ) · g = g −1 X, Y g .

Let X+ and X− denote the GIT quotients V //± G associated to the characters det+1 and det−1 ,
respectively. Then
                                                                                 
             X+ = tot S+ ⊗ p∗ F ∨ → Gr(r, E),      X− = tot(S− ⊗ p∗ E) → Gr r, F ∨ ,
                                               
where p : Gr(r,
               E) → B resp. p : Gr r, F ∨ → B denotes the relative Grassmannian of r-planes in
E resp. F ∨ and S± denotes the rank-r tautological bundle on the associated Grassmann bundle.
In other words, the fiber of
                                          Gr(r, E) → B
over a closed point b ∈ B is the Grassmannian Gr(r, Eb ) where Eb is the fiber of E → B over b.
   The birational map
                                         f : X− 99K X+
is a relative version of what is known as a Grassmann flop. We construct a resolution of this map in
Section 2.3 following [9], and compute the associated Fourier–Mukai transform in K-theory.
