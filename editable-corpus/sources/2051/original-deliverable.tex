\paragraph{Editorial problem boundary.}
Prove only Theorem 3.12 below for a closed connected orientable smooth manifold and Morse--Smale vector field whose closed orbits have orientable unstable bundles, with the printed complex flat bundle and arbitrary Hermitian metric. The exact determinant-line Ray--Singer/dynamical Milnor metric comparison, negative vector-field sign, full untwisted Case 1 fusion diagram and characteristic-current variation are retained. No acyclicity/unitarity assumption is added; omitted twisted/nonorientable extensions are outside this selected claim.
\paragraph{Difficulty (editorial): H2.}
The cited Franks standardisation and Bismut--Zhang theorem do not by themselves compare the dynamical Milnor metric with the analytic metric for the original Morse--Smale flow. The author constructs the untwisted determinant-line fusion comparison through orbit destruction and proves the matching variation of the characteristic current. Tracking those metric and current contributions, including the negative-vector-field sign, is the nonroutine step that makes the final comparison formula hold.
