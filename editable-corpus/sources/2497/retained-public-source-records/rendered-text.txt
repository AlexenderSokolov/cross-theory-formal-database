Conjecture 1.5. [1, Conjecture 1.16] There is a constant C := C(d) > 0 such that for any field K and linear subspace
W ⊆ Kn1 ⊗K · · · ⊗K Knd , we have
                                           SRK (W) ≤ C SRK (W ⊗K K).
   Conjecture 1.5 was only confirmed for d = 2 in [1, Theorem 1.17]. In Theorem 5.11, we resolve Conjecture 1.5 with
C = d/2 + 1 for all d ≥ 2, by associating a tensor TW of order d + 2 to W such that SRK (TW ) = SRK (W).

2.1 Ranks of tensors
