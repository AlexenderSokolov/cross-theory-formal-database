3.4 Reducibility conditions
See Kac–Kazhdan [3]. Set

                                                        κ = k + 2.

The Verma module V (m, k − m) is reducible if and only if at least one of the following relations holds:

 (a) m − l + 1 + (a − 1)κ = 0,
 (b) m + l + 1 − aκ = 0,
 (c) κ = 0,

where l, a ∈ Z>0 . If (m, κ) satisfies exactly one of the conditions (a), (b), then V (m, k − m) contains a
unique proper submodule, and this submodule is generated by a singular vector of degree (la, l(a − 1))
for condition (a) and of degree (l(a − 1), la) for condition (b).
