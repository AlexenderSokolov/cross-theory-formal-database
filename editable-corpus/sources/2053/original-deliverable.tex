\paragraph{Editorial problem boundary.}
Prove only Theorem 4.1 below for a polarized absolutely rigid linear submanifold of a stratum of translation surfaces, with the exact author-defined Hermitian volume and its push-forward measure on projectivisation. The conclusion is \(\mu(\mathbf P\mathcal M)\in\mathbf Q\pi^{\dim\mathcal M}\). The entire curvature-volume calculation and finite-cover/graph-modification/resolution claim are retained. Separate lattice-point counting, Masur--Veech normalization and tiling asymptotics are surrounding context and are not selected; their sketch-labelled arguments are not claimed as complete primary proofs.
\paragraph{Difficulty (editorial): H3.}
Kollár's cited curvature-current theorem applies after a suitable extension of the tautological line bundle; it does not itself construct that extension for the noncompact linear submanifold. The author first proves the exact Hermitian volume-to-curvature identity, then constructs a ramified cover and a graph modification followed by resolution that place the line subbundle in the required setting. The degree bookkeeping and integral characteristic class then close the printed rational-power-of-pi volume conclusion.
