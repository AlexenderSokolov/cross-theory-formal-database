tri-tronquée solution of PI (see [22]), and the non-linear Stokes phenomena on Stokes curves are analyzed
by [17, 24, 36].

3 Topological recursion and quantum curve theorem
In this section we review the Eynard–Orantin’s topological recursion [16] for our spectral curve (2.9),
and formulate our main theorem.

3.1 Topological recursion
The topological recursion is an algorithm associating some differential forms Wg,n and numbers Fg given
the following source data:
   • A plane curve (C, x, y): C is a compact Riemann surface, x, y : C → P1 are meromorphic functions.

     the diagonal, and satisfying some normalization conditions.
