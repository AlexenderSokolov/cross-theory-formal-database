2 Background: dynamical quantum groups
In this section, we will give a brief overview of the classical extremal projector and its relation to
dynamical quantum groups through the category of Harish-Chandra bimodules.

2.1 General setup
For the rest of the paper, we work over an algebraically closed field C of characteristic zero. All
categories and functors we will consider are C-linear; likewise, unless otherwise stated, all tensor
products are taken over C. Often, we will use the algebra of polynomial functions C[h̄] in variable
h̄ and denote by ModC[h̄] the corresponding monoidal category of C[h̄]-modules with the standard
tensor product ⊗C[h̄] over C[h̄]. For any vector space V over C, we denote by V [h̄] := V ⊗C C[h̄].
    Throughout this paper, we work with locally presentable categories (we refer to [1] and [8, Section
2] for more details). We will also use Deligne’s tensor product of locally presentable categories
introduced in [13].

2.2 Harish-Chandra bimodules
For details and proofs in a more general setting, we refer the reader to [30, Section 2].
   Let G be an aﬀine algebraic group and g be its Lie algebra with bracket [ , ].

Definition 2.1. The asymptotic universal enveloping algebra Uh̄ (g) of g is a tensor algebra over C[h̄]
generated by the vector space g with the relations xy − yx = h̄[x, y], x, y ∈ g. The non-asymptotic
universal enveloping algebra U(g) is a C-algebra generated by g with the relations xy − yx = [x, y].

Remark 2.2. In principle, most of the cited literature deals with the non-asymptotic version U(g).
However, unless specified otherwise, all the constructions and proofs can be translated mutatis mu-
tandis to the asymptotic setting.

Definition 2.3 ([7]). An asymptotic Harish-Chandra bimodule is a left Uh̄ (g)-module X in the
category Rep(G). In other words, it has a structure of a G-representation and a left Uh̄ (g)-module
such that the action morphism Uh̄ (g) ⊗ X → X is a homomorphism of G-representations. The
category of Harish-Chandra bimodules is denoted by HCh̄ (G).

    In what follows, we will refer to asymptotic Harish-Chandra bimodules as simply Harish-Chandra
bimodules.
    There is a natural right Uh̄ (g)-module structure on any Harish-Chandra bimodule X. Namely,
for ξ ∈ g, define
                                     xξ := ξx − h̄ adξ (x), x ∈ X,                            (2.1)
and extend it to a right Uh̄ (g)-action. Therefore, the category HCh̄ (G) is a subcategory of Uh̄ (g)-
bimodules, hence is equipped with a tensor structure X ⊗HCh̄ (G) Y := X ⊗Uh̄ (g) Y .

Remark 2.4. The subcategory of HCh̄ (G) where h̄ acts by identity is identified with the standard
category of Harish-Chandra bimodules [6, Definition 5.2]. The one where h̄ acts by zero can be
identified with the category of G-equivariant quasi-coherent sheaves on g∗ .

   There is a natural functor of the so-called free (right) Harish-Chandra bimodules

                           free : Rep(G) → HCh̄ (G),           V 7→ V ⊗ Uh̄ (g),

such that the right Uh̄ (g)-action is free and the G-action is diagonal. By [30, equation (28)], it is
isomorphic to the functor of free left Harish-Chandra bimodules V 7→ Uh̄ (g) ⊗ V ; however, in what
follows, we will use the right version. One can check that this functor is monoidal. In fact, all
Harish-Chandra bimodules can be “constructed” from the free ones.

Proposition 2.5 ([30, Proposition 2.7]). The category HCh̄ (G) is generated by free(V ) for V ∈
Rep(G).
