\begin{conjecture}\cite[Conjecture 7.1]{HPW}\label{conj:mainresult}
Let $w \in S_n$ be a permutation. The CDG generators are a diagonal Gr\"obner basis for $I_w$ if and only if $w$ avoids all eight of the patterns \[
13254, 21543, 214635, 215364, 215634, 241635, 315264, 4261735.
\]
\end{conjecture}
