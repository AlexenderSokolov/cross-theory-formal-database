\item A regular ring of finite dimension has finite global dimension. invoked at proof lines 21841-21843. Provides the finite global dimension of A.
\item Over a ring of finite global dimension every module has finite tor dimension. invoked at proof lines 21844-21846. Gives the finite tor dimension of B.
\item A finite type ring map out of a Noetherian ring is pseudo-coherent. invoked at proof lines 21847-21848. Gives the pseudo-coherence half of perfectness.

\begin{lemma}
\label{lemma-regular-perfect-ring-map}
Let $A \to B$ be a finite type ring map with $A$ a regular ring
of finite dimension. Then $A \to B$ is perfect.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By
Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-finite-gl-dim-finite-dim-regular}
the assumption on $A$ means that $A$ has finite global dimension.
Hence every module has finite tor dimension, see
Lemma \ref{lemma-finite-gl-dim-tor-dimension},
in particular $B$ does. By
Lemma \ref{lemma-Noetherian-pseudo-coherent-ring-map}
the map is pseudo-coherent.
\end{proof}
