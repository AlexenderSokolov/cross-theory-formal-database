\begin{lemma}
\label{lemma-characterize-excellent}
Let $X$ be a scheme. The following are equivalent
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is excellent, and
\item $X$ is quasi-excellent and universally catenary.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Assume (1). Since excellent rings are quasi-excellent, it is clear
that $X$ is quasi-excellent. Since excellent rings are universally
catenary, it also follows that $X$ is universally catenary by
Lemma \ref{lemma-universally-catenary-local}. Assume (2).
Let $x \in X$. Since $X$ is quasi-excellent there exists
an affine open neighbourhood $x \in U \subset X$
such that $\mathcal{O}_X(U)$ is quasi-excellent. Since
$X$ is universally category, the ring $\mathcal{O}_X(U)$ is also
universally catenary by Lemma \ref{lemma-universally-catenary-local},
whence excellent.
\end{proof}
