\noindent\textit{Allowed standard prerequisites (editorial).}\begin{itemize}
\item Stalks of a sheaf of modules and the localization maps along specializations: for a specialization x' in the closure of \{x\} the stalk at x' of a module sheaf receives the stalk at x by localization at a multiplicative set. Used at proof lines 669-671, where F\_\{x'\} is identified as a localization of the module F\_x. Gives the reduction of the statement to the vanishing of a localization of a zero module.
\item The support of F is the set of points with nonzero stalk, so its complement is the locus where the stalk vanishes. Used at statement line 663 and proof line 671. Turns the conclusion 'closed under specialization' into the equivalent statement that the complement is closed under generalization.
\end{itemize}\medskip
\begin{lemma}
\label{lemma-support-closed-specialization}
Let $X$ be a scheme.
Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a quasi-coherent sheaf on $X$.
The support of $\mathcal{F}$ is closed under specialization.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If $x' \leadsto x$ is a specialization and $\mathcal{F}_x = 0$
then $\mathcal{F}_{x'}$ is zero, as $\mathcal{F}_{x'}$ is a localization
of the module $\mathcal{F}_x$. Hence the complement of
$\text{Supp}(\mathcal{F})$ is closed under generalization.
\end{proof}
