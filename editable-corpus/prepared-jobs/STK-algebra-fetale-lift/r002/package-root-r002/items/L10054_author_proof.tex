\begin{lemma}
\label{lemma-formally-etale-lift-infinitesimal}
Let $R \to S$ be a ring map. Let $J \subset S$ be an ideal such
that $R \to S/J$ is surjective; let $I \subset R$ be the kernel.
If $R \to S$ is formally \'etale, then $R/I^n \to S/J^n$ is an
isomorphism for all $n$ and
$\bigoplus I^n/I^{n + 1} \to \bigoplus J^n/J^{n + 1}$
is an isomorphism of graded rings.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Using the lifting property inductively we find dotted arrows
$$
\xymatrix{
S \ar[r] \ar@{-->}[rd] & S/J = R/I \\
R \ar[r] \ar[u] & R/I^2 \ar[u]
}
\quad
\xymatrix{
S \ar[r] \ar@{-->}[rd] & R/I^2 \\
R \ar[r] \ar[u] & R/I^3 \ar[u]
}
\quad
\xymatrix{
S \ar[r] \ar@{-->}[rd] & R/I^3 \\
R \ar[r] \ar[u] & R/I^4 \ar[u]
}
$$
The corresponding maps $S/J^n \to R/I^n$ are isomorphisms
since the compositions $S/J^n \to R/I^n \to S/J^n$ are
(inductively) the identity by the uniqueness in the lifting
property of formally \'etale ring maps.
\end{proof}
