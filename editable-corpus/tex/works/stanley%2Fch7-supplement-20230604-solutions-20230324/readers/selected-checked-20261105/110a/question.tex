(a) [2+] Let $\kappa(w)$ denote the number of cycles of $w\in \mathfrak{S}_n$. Show that
\[
P_n(q):=\sum_{w}q^{\kappa(w(1,2,\ldots,n))}=\frac{1}{n(n+1)}\left((q+n)_{n+1}-(q)_{n+1}\right).
\]
where $w$ ranges over all $(n-1)!$ $n$-cycles in $\mathfrak{S}_n$ and $w(1,2,\ldots,n)$ denotes the product of $w$ with the $n$-cycle $(1,2,\ldots,n)$. For instance,
\[
\sum_{\rho(w)=(3)}q^{\kappa(w(1,2,3))}=\frac{1}{12}\left((q+3)_4-(q)_4\right)=q^3+q.
\]
\textsc{Hint.} Use Exercise 7.70.
