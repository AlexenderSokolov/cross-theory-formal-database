(a) [$3$--] Let $A_1,\ldots,A_k$ be subsets of $[n]$ satisfying $\bigcup A_i=[n]$ and $A_i\cap A_j=\{1\}$ for all $i<j$. Set $a_i=\#A_i$. Show that the symmetric function
\[
G_{a_1,\ldots,a_k} := \sum_{w_1\in\mathfrak{S}_{A_1}}\cdots\sum_{w_k\in\mathfrak{S}_{A_k}} p_{\rho(w_1\cdots w_k)} \tag{15}
\]
is equal to $\prod (a_i-1)!$ times the coefficient of $x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_k^{a_k}$ in
\[
\Big(\sum_{i_1,\ldots,i_k\ge 1} p_{i_1+\cdots+i_k-k+1}x_1^{i_1}\cdots x_k^{i_k}\Big)H(x_1)\cdots H(x_k),
\]
where $H(t)=\sum_{n\ge 0}h_nt^n$.
