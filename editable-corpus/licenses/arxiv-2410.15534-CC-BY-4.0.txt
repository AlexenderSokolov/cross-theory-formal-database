WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/167136_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Theorem 4.11 (native source lines 1728-1730, printed on page 30 of
  the published version: the surface Y_{pi/6} has Morse index two and nullity five) together with the
  article's complete proof of it (lines 1732-1930, one proof environment of 199 lines, adjacent to the
  statement); and verbatim the statements, and only the statements, of the results that proof invokes:
  Proposition 3.5 (lines 646-675), Corollary 3.6 (lines 789-827), Proposition 3.7 (lines 891-943),
  Proposition 4.1 (lines 1094-1129) and Lemma 4.7 (lines 1451-1461), together with the retained section and
  subsection headings (lines 192-195 and line 1727). Every retained line is the article's own line,
  unchanged; nothing is omitted, so the package ships no omission record.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper keeps the article's four theorem-like environments on one shared
  counter but without the article's section-based prefix, so the retained items print as Proposition 1,
  Corollary 2, Proposition 3, Proposition 4, Lemma 5 and Theorem 6 where the article prints Proposition
  3.5, Corollary 3.6, Proposition 3.7, Proposition 4.1, Lemma 4.7 and Theorem 4.11; the retained headings
  print as Section 1, Subsection 1.1 and Subsection 1.2 where the article prints Section 3, Subsection 3.1
  and Subsection 4.6. Every display of the retained lines is unnumbered, so no equation number is
  renumbered.
* sources/167136/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file main.tex except that the pipeline's
  mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added, removed or
  changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only; the article itself is typeset with the AMS amsart class.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image file and no external artwork: the retained spans contain no figure environment and no
image-inclusion command, and the article's single illustration is not retained. No bibliography and no
bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation. No publisher class or style file, no upstream
script, no font and no unrelated artwork from any other source. No content was deleted from any retained
span.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
