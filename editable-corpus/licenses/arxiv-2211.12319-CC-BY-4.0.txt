WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/8071_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces,
  verbatim and in the article's own notation, the article's Proposition 4.5.1
  and its complete proof (native source lines 1687-1691 and 1693-1715), together
  with verbatim excerpts of the article's own abstract (lines 41-55), its
  subsection heading and definition of a generic representation (lines
  370-377), its subsection heading and definition of a polynomial generic
  representation with its degree (lines 714 and 720-726), its section and
  subsection headings and its definition of a subset of a polynomial
  representation (lines 1523-1526 and 1533-1537), its Noetherianity theorem
  (lines 1539-1544), its subsection heading for the reduction (line 1685), and
  its boxed remark recording the consequence of the proposition (lines
  1717-1723).
* sources/8071/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as
  distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the
  e-print file tensorrestriction221121b.tex except that the pipeline's
  mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming the file it
  was read from ("% concatenated from tensorrestriction221121b.tex"); nothing
  else is added, removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These
  are editorial formatting only.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No figure, diagram or other artwork: the retained excerpts contain no
graphics-inclusion command and the package declares no asset dependency. No
bibliography and no bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation.
No publisher class or style file: the article's own style file all2021.sty is
neither shipped nor input, and the macros and theorem environments that the
retained lines use are restated in the wrapper. No upstream script, no font and
no unrelated artwork from any other source.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or
re-derived, and the wrapper adds only the editorial material described in the
document's own scope paragraph.
