1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one theorem or proposition of the Work, delivered with its
complete original proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the author team's own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex at the exact line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json (method "exact_tex");
  * the two occurrences of the forward cross-reference to Section 6 of the Work at source0.tex lines 140 and 152, delivered as \href links to the version PDF with the printed number retained (method "reference_only_adaptation", the adapted lines are set out in delivery-evidence.json);
  * the author team's own macro and \newtheorem declarations and its own \thetheorem definition, copied verbatim;
  * the author team's own bibliography entries, transcribed verbatim from the e-print file Horospheres_in_RACGs_Paper.bbl with the article's printed bibliography numbers;
  * the author team's own numbering, so that every printed section, subsection, theorem, proposition, lemma, corollary, definition, remark and equation number of the delivered unit is produced by the author team's own counters;

2. EDITORIAL ADDITIONS (NOT AUTHOR MATERIAL)
--------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus;
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered item;
  * an editorial font substitution: the Work sets its text and mathematics in the newtx faces, which are not present in the pinned runtime, so the delivered unit is typeset in the runtime Latin Modern faces;

These additions are formatting, provenance and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical statement, proof
step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or altered, and the
printed slips of the Work are kept literal.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK

The Work's twelve picture assets, which occur only in its Section 6 (Examples), are not redistributed and are not part of the delivered unit; no artwork of any kind is included in this package.
