WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/168136_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Lemma 4.5 (native source lines 376-381, printed on page 9: for a
  tangle T_A given by a sub-diagram of A_1, any colouring by a medial quandle forces n=d and m=e) together
  with the article's complete proof of it (lines 383-425, one proof environment of 43 lines, adjacent to the
  statement); and verbatim the statements, and only the statements, of the results that proof invokes: the
  definition of the maps f_xy (line 342, printed as Definition 4.2), the proposition listing their algebraic
  properties (lines 344-354, printed as Proposition 4.3) and the theorem that the group they generate is
  abelian with automorphic elements (lines 364-368, printed as Theorem 4.4). Every retained line is the
  article's own line, unchanged, apart from the declared adaptation recorded next.
* Declared adaptation, and the only difference from the source lines: the retained source spans contain four
  figure environments and four image-inclusion commands; the package keeps the four figure environments
  with their placement, their captions and their labels, but omits the four image-inclusion commands
  (naming the image files TA.png, TAtransform.png, A1twostr.png and dissectofTA.png), so exactly four lines
  of the retained spans differ from the source and no other line does. No image is shipped, every figure
  cross-reference of the retained lines still resolves through the retained labels, and the package declares
  no asset dependency. These four omitted commands are recorded in the fidelity receipts bound to the
  counted statement and to the counted proof, whose roles are exactly those two bodies, and no other
  adaptation is declared anywhere else in the package.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper keeps the article's theorem-like environments on one shared counter
  but without the article's section-based prefix, so the retained items print as Definition 1, Proposition
  2, Theorem 3 and Lemma 4, the retained figure environments print as Figure 1 to Figure 4, and the
  retained displayed equations print as (1) to (5); the article prints the same items with the numbering of
  its own full document, which is not reproduced here.
* sources/168136/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file main.tex except that the pipeline's
  mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added, removed or
  changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages including the standard float package that the
  article itself loads, and this notice. These are editorial formatting only. The article was typeset with
  the standard article class.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image and no artwork: all image-inclusion commands of the retained spans were omitted as declared above,
so the package ships no graphics file and declares no asset dependency. No bibliography and no bibliography
entry: no retained line contains a citation. No publisher class or style file, no upstream script, no font
and no unrelated artwork from any other source. Apart from the declared adaptation, no content was deleted
from any retained span.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
