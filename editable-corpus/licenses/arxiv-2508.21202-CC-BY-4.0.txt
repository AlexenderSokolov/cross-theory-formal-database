1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one proposition of the Work, delivered with its complete
original proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the author's own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex
    (sha256 9eb793fda3e6b4c3cbe45d42738c2a49f933263eec5290909b3af2302fa063cb) at the exact line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json, all of them under the
    exact method "exact_tex": source lines 345-779 (the whole of the author's section 2, "What is MV?",
    retained as the necessary complete source-local core and not counted), source lines 780-784 (the counted
    statement, the unlabelled proposition printed as Proposition 10 in the version of record) and source
    lines 786-902 (the complete original author proof of that proposition, 117 lines, the longest proof
    block of the Work);
  * the author's own abstract, reproduced verbatim as the front matter of the delivered item (source0.tex
    lines 79-84; it is the Work's own abstract and carries no citation);
  * the author's own theorem declarations as written by LyX for this manuscript, copied verbatim from
    source0.tex lines 26-50 (\numberwithin for equation and figure, the plain, remark and definition
    theorem styles, \newtheorem for thm, fact, cor, prop, lem and defn sharing the single thm counter, and
    the starred claim*), its lyxlist environment (source0.tex lines 34-40), its environment names
    (source0.tex lines 67-73), its \usepackage[authoryear]{natbib} (source0.tex line 17) and its
    \PassOptionsToPackage{normalem}{ulem} and \usepackage{ulem} (source0.tex lines 18-19), re-declared in
    the wrapper preamble because the wrapper is an amsart document of this corpus rather than the author's
    own preamble;
  * the author's own bibliography header declarations \natexlab, \url and \doi and the author's own
    bibliography entries, transcribed verbatim from the Work's in-source thebibliography (source0.tex lines
    2056-2321, which holds 45 entries); exactly the 16 entries cited inside the retained spans are carried,

    Solovay1970AMO, HjorthU2 and KunenST, each with the author's own printed author-year label; the 29

2. EDITORIAL ADDITIONS AND BOUNDED ADAPTATIONS (NOT AUTHOR MATERIAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus;
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered
    item;
  * the editorial counter initialisation \setcounter{section}{1} and \setcounter{thm}{2}, which precedes
    the delivered spans and reproduces the author's own numbering (see point 1); it changes no text;
  * one typographic declaration, \bibpunct[,]{[}{]}{,}{a}{,}{}: the e-print source carries no \bibpunct,
    yet the version of record prints citations in square brackets with a comma before the year, so this
    declaration is added in order that the delivered citations show the published punctuation
    ([Author, Year] for \citep and Author [Year] for \citet). It changes no number, symbol, statement or
    proof step.
  * the author's preamble loads that nothing in the retained spans uses are not reproduced: ae, aecompl,
    fontenc, inputenc, babel, geometry, setspace are font, encoding and page-geometry matter supplied by the
    wrapper, and qtree, bussproofs, pdflscape, tikz and tikz-cd are used by the Work only outside the
    retained spans (its two tikzpictures are at source0.tex lines 1640-1671 and 1707-1738, both far outside
    the retained spans, and the retained spans contain no tikzpicture, no prooftree and no landscape
    environment). No reference of the retained spans points outside them: every \ref of the delivered text
    resolves inside the delivered text, so the delivery evidence carries no replacement entry and no
    reference adaptation of any kind.

These additions are formatting, provenance, numbering and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical
statement, proof step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or
altered, and the printed slips of the Work are kept literal.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK
---------------------------------------
The Work's publisher class file is not used, not shipped and not redistributed: the Work is itself an
amsart manuscript (source0.tex line 4 reads \documentclass[oneside,english]{amsart}) and the e-print
archive holds no .cls, .sty or .bbl member, so the wrapper is an editorial AMS formatting layer. The Work
contains no \includegraphics at all, no figure environment, no external graphic file and no artwork; no
retained span of this package contains an \includegraphics command, and no figure or artwork asset exists
in this package or is redistributed with it. The delivery evidence of this item carries no
"bounded_source_comparison" body and no fidelity receipt of any kind; the only fidelity classes it uses are
"exact_tex" for all three delivered spans, so no verbatim text of the Work was omitted from a retained span.
