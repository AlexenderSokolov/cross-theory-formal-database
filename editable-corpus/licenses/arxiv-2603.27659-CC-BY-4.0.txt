EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is the standard LaTeX article class at 12pt, which is not
a publisher class, and that class is not shipped with this package. The wrapper
(amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is this lane's
editorial formatting. It restates the paper's own ten theorem-like declarations
with their section-relative counter, its section-relative equation numbering and
its own mathematical shorthand macros, all copied verbatim, so that the printed
numbers of the delivered statements are the paper's own: the retained sections
print as Sections 2 to 6, the counted statement as Theorem 3.1, the retained
lemmas, definitions, propositions and corollaries with the paper's numbers, and
the numbered displays as the paper's (2.1) to (6.1). One editorial counter
restores the paper's section number before the first retained section. Those
formatting differences are editorial and alter no formula, symbol, hypothesis or
argument.
