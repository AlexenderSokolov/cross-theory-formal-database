WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
--------------------------
This package reproduces, under the CC BY 4.0 licence above, text that the named
author wrote and that arXiv distributes for this version:

  * items/8048_author_proof.tex — an editorial amsart assembly of the paper's own
    LaTeX source lines. The counted statement (source0.tex lines 331-337) and the
    counted proof (source0.tex lines 675-1128) are the author's text verbatim, and
    the retained body (source0.tex lines 141-330, 338-674 and 1129-1834) is
    likewise the author's text verbatim, except for the five graphics-inclusion
    commands identified below. Nothing was translated, corrected, re-typeset by
    hand, or re-derived. Line-by-line binding of every retained span against
    cached-originals/source0.tex is carried in delivery-evidence.json.
  * sources/8048/source0.tex — the prep lane's mechanical concatenation of the
    paper's own .tex source, byte-identical to cached-originals/source0.tex.
  * The thirty-three bibliography entries printed in the delivered document were

ASSETS, GRAPHICS AND INCLUDES
-----------------------------
The paper contains five graphics-inclusion commands inside five figure
environments, and no PDF-page inclusion, input or include directive. All five lie
inside the retained body, so exactly those five were omitted, and the delivered TeX
contains no \includegraphics, \includepdf, \input or \include in active text. The
five figure environments themselves, with their captions, subcaptions, side
captions and labels, are retained verbatim, so the delivered document still prints
the paper's figure captions and every reference to a figure still resolves, but no
image appears. No image, plot or data file is copied into or redistributed with
this package; the package declares no asset dependency (manifest and
delivery-evidence both carry an empty asset-dependency list) and the compile
receipt registers no figure dependency. The paper's tables are native TeX and are
retained and printed as tables.

EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is article with the options a4paper, 10pt and english,
loading fullpage, fancyhdr, subfig together with subcaption, svg, helvet,
pgfplots, empheq, cases and the cite package. The wrapper (amsart, 10pt, A4,
geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is this lane's editorial
formatting. It reproduces the paper's own macro block, the paper's own ten
theorem-like environment declarations with their section-relative counters and its
assumption counter format A1, A2, so the delivered statements keep the paper's own
numbers, and it loads the packages the retained text needs: graphicx for the
resize boxes, float for the H-placed table, booktabs and multirow for the tables,
subcaption for the subfloats and side captions, algorithm with algpseudocode for
the algorithm floats, xcolor and cleveref. The paper's loads of fullpage,
fancyhdr, subfig, svg, helvet, pgfplots, empheq, cases and cite are therefore
replaced or omitted; the effect is a page-layout difference and, for the cite
package alone, a difference in how runs of citations are sorted and compressed on
the page. No formula, symbol, hypothesis, argument, statement number or proof
number changes.
