  article's own notation, the article's proposition hölder (native source lines 1412-1414) together with the article's
  complete proof of it (lines 1415-1449, one proof environment of 35 lines). Retained uncounted as the source-local context the proof needs: lines 490-492; lines 1187-1191; lines 1373-1376.
  Every retained line is the article's own line, unchanged; nothing was omitted from any retained span, so the package contains no omission record and declares no adaptation.

* sources/195872/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is the article's own source as the pipeline concatenated it, byte identical to the e-print files apart from the single header comment line naming them; nothing else is added, removed or changed.
