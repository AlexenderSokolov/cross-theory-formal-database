1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one theorem of the Work, delivered with its complete original
proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the authors' own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex at the
    exact line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json, namely source lines 173--1133 (the whole body from
    the Introduction to the line immediately preceding the counted theorem), 1134--1143 (the counted theorem)
    and 1144--1192 (its complete proof);
  * the authors' own title, author, address and e-mail lines, Mathematics Subject Classification line,
    keywords and abstract, reproduced verbatim from source lines 118--131, 143, 146 and 150--164;
  * the authors' own theorem-environment block, including the unnumbered named environments that the article
    never uses, from source lines 78--101, and the authors' own macro block, character-fusion construction,
    cup-dot symbols and operator names from source lines 27--61 and 103--110;
  * the authors' own bibliography entries for the 50 works cited by the delivered text, transcribed verbatim
    from the article's own inline thebibliography at source lines 1650--1820 in the authors' own order and
    with the authors' printed bibliography numbers (the commented-out entries of that list are not printed by
    the published version and are not transcribed);
  * the authors' own numbering, so that every printed section, definition, lemma, proposition, corollary,
    remark, example, theorem, equation and citation number of the delivered unit coincides with the published
    version (the counted theorem is printed as Theorem 4.10, as in the published version).

2. EDITORIAL ADDITIONS (NOT AUTHOR MATERIAL)
--------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus, together
    with the two packages this unit needs beyond it (amsthm and color, the latter for the authors' own
    editorial colour mark);
  * six cross-references of the Introduction, at source lines 221, 224, 226 and 227, that point at material
    outside this unit, delivered as \href links to the abstract page of this exact version with the printed
    numbers retained (method "reference_only_adaptation", the adapted lines are set out in
    delivery-evidence.json);
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered
    item;
  * the omission of the article's \tableofcontents call and of its page-geometry and penalty settings, since
    the delivered unit stops inside Section 4 and the wrapper fixes the page geometry of this corpus;
  * the page-comparison witness described in section 4 below, which is kept outside this package.

These additions are formatting, provenance and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical statement, proof
step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or altered, and the
printed slips of the Work are kept literal, including the authors' editorial colour mark printed at the end of
the proof of Lemma 2.7.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK
---------------------------------------
The Work contains no figure, no table, no diagram and no artwork of any kind, and none is included in this
package; the e-print source contains no \includegraphics and no \input. The Work is itself an amsart article,
so no publisher class file, publisher typesetting, upstream script or font of a publisher is used, shipped or
redistributed, and no part of a publisher PDF is redistributed. The bibliography transcription redistributes
only the authors' own reference list as it appears in their e-print.
