WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/179199_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's completeness proposition (native source lines 1524-1529: the
  orthonormality relation satisfied by the discrete eigenfunctions together with the continuous family of
  Eisenstein-type functions) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines 1532-1613, one proof
  environment of 82 lines, adjacent to the statement). No further source-local context is needed: every
  cross-reference of every retained line resolves inside the two delivered spans. Every retained line is the
  article's own line, unchanged; nothing was omitted from any retained span, so the package carries no
  omission record and declares no adaptation.
* Numbering as delivered: the retained environment prints in this document as Proposition 1, whereas the
  article prints the same item with the numbering of its own full document; the numbered displays of the
  retained lines print consecutively in order of appearance, whereas the article numbers its equations per
  section.
* sources/179199/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file heatkernelPSL2Z_arXiv_may26.tex except
  that the pipeline's mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is
  added, removed or changed. The article's non-ASCII accented characters and dashes lie outside every
  retained span.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only; the article itself is typeset with the AMS amsart class.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image and no artwork: no retained line contains a figure environment or an image-inclusion command, and
the retained text contains no TikZ or drawing code; the package declares no asset dependency. No
bibliography and no bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation. No publisher class or style
file, no upstream script, no font and no unrelated artwork from any other source. No content was deleted
from any retained span.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
