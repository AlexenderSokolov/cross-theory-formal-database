WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/8069_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces,
  verbatim and in the article's own notation, the article's Theorem 4.3 (the
  upper bound meg(G) <= 9 fes(G) + |L(G)| - 8) and its complete proof (native
  source lines 384-386 and 387-420), together with verbatim excerpts of the
  article's own abstract (lines 67-73), its definition of monitoring
  edge-geodetic sets with the notation meg(G) (lines 84-91), its Lemma 2.1 and
  Lemma 2.3 with the article's own proofs (lines 139-143 and 159-164), its
  Theorem 3.1 with the article's own proof (lines 179-183), the statement of
  its Theorem 3.4 (lines 201-203), the heading of its section 4 with the
  definition of the feedback edge set number (lines 345-346), its core-vertex
  terminology paragraph (line 349), its Lemma 4.1 with the introducing sentence
  (lines 360-363), its Lemma 4.2 with the article's own proof (lines 366-371),
  and its sentence on the cases fes(G) = 0 and fes(G) = 1 (line 373).
  The retained text cites exactly two works, and the wrapper reproduces those
  two entries of the article's own bibliography, transcribed verbatim with the
  article's printed numbers as labels: [7] L. Epstein, A. Levin and
  G. J. Woeginger, The (weighted) metric dimension of graphs: hard and easy
  cases, Algorithmica 72(4), 1130-1171, 2015; and [15] L. Kellerhals and
  T. Koana, Parameterized complexity of geodetic set, IPEC 2020, LIPIcs 180,
  20:1-20:14, 2020.
* sources/8069/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as
  distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the
  e-print file main_revision.tex except that the pipeline's mechanical
  concatenation prepends one header comment line naming the file it was read
  from ("% concatenated from main_revision.tex"); nothing else is added,
  removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These
  are editorial formatting only.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No figure, diagram or other artwork. The article's figure environments (its
parameter-relation diagram and its graph illustrations) lie outside every
retained span, the retained lines contain no graphics-inclusion command, and
the package declares no asset dependency. No bibliography beyond the two cited
entries reproduced in the wrapper. No publisher class or style file, no
upstream script, no font and no unrelated artwork from any other source.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or
re-derived, and the wrapper adds only the editorial material described in the
document's own scope paragraph.
