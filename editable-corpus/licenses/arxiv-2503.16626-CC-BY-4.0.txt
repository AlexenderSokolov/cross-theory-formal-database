WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/175132_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's main theorem (native source lines 382-384: the space of continuous
  functions on an F_d-compact of countable spectral height admits an equivalent LUR norm that is lower
  semicontinuous for the pointwise topology) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines
  386-457, one proof environment of 72 lines, adjacent to the statement); and verbatim the statements, and
  only the statements, of the results that proof invokes, namely the metrizability criterion for fully
  closed mappings (lines 185-192), the countable-decomposition characterization of LUR renormings (lines
  202-207), the metric projection proposition (lines 123-125) and the finite-branching lemma (lines
  347-349), together with the section heading that introduces the construction (line 236). Every retained
  line is the article's own line, unchanged; nothing was omitted from any retained span, so the package
  carries no omission record and declares no adaptation.
* A four-entry bibliography. The retained lines cite exactly four works, and their entries are transcribed
  verbatim from the article's own bibliography block in the bundled source (its entries fedFC, holmes_opt,
  mot97 and motv), with the short editorial labels Fed06, Hol72, MOT97 and MOTV09; the transcription is
  recorded in the item's reference mapping. No other bibliography entry is transcribed, and no retained
  line cites anything else.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper declares the retained theorem-like environments (theorem,
  proposition, lemma, corollary and claim) on separate counters so that cross-references name the right kind
  of statement, and they print as Proposition 1, Proposition 2, Theorem 3, Lemma 4 and Theorem 5 in order of
  appearance, with the single unnamed claim inside the counted proof printing as Claim 1, whereas the
  article prints the same items with the numbering of its own full document; the numbered displays of the
  retained lines print consecutively in order of appearance, whereas the article numbers its equations per
  section.
* sources/175132/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file f-decomposable.tex except that the
  pipeline's mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added,
  removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages including the standard enumitem package that the
  article itself loads for its list spacing, and this notice. These are editorial formatting only; the
  article was typeset with the standard article class.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image and no artwork: no retained line contains a figure environment or an image-inclusion command, and
the retained text contains no TikZ or drawing code; the package declares no asset dependency. No publisher
class or style file, no upstream script, no font and no unrelated artwork from any other source. No content
was deleted from any retained span.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
