Version substitution. The newest version of this article, v4, is withdrawn by the author: its abstract page states that the paper has been withdrawn, records v4 as a one-kilobyte submission marked withdrawn, and adds that there is no licence for that version because it is withdrawn; the v4 e-print and PDF endpoints serve the withdrawal notice page instead of the article. This package therefore delivers the newest intact version, v3, as LANE RECIPE section 7 prescribes. The withdrawal evidence is saved with the item as abs-v4-withdrawn.html and the two saved v4 endpoint responses, and is recorded in STATUS-LEAD.json and SOURCE_MAPPING.json.

Components delivered by this package. Verbatim source lines of the article for the counted statement, for the counted proof, and for the statement of the retained single-covering lemma, copied line for line from the article's own TeX source and bundled as sources/153405/source0.tex; the delivered item items/153405_author_proof.tex; and this notice. An editorial AMS amsart wrapper supplies formatting, the article's own environment declarations for Lemma and Proposition, the article's macros and its definition of the modulus divisor, together with the xy package used for the article's commutative diagrams.

Components omitted. The remainder of the article, including its main theorems, its other sections and its remaining proofs; the article's bibliography, since no retained line carries a citation and no reference entry is delivered; and all artwork. No retained span contains any picture, diagram or graphical inclusion, and no image asset is delivered or redistributed; the commutative diagrams of the retained spans are drawn by the retained diagram code itself. The article's own class and style files, its fonts, its bibliography databases and any other publisher or upstream file are neither shipped nor redistributed, and no such file is present in this package; in particular the Springer Nature class file that accompanies the e-print is not used by the article's own source and is not present here.

Retention method. Every retained span is recorded in delivery-evidence.json with method exact_tex; no retained line is altered, re-flowed or partially omitted, and no bounded-source-comparison body exists.
