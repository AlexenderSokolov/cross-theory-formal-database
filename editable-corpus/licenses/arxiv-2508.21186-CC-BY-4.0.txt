1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one theorem of the Work, delivered with its complete original
proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the authors' own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex at the exact
    line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json: the whole body from source line 62 to line 400 is delivered as
    context and lines 401-409 (the counted Theorem 4.1) and 411-415 (its complete proof) are delivered verbatim
    (method "exact_tex") as the counted unit;
  * the context span is delivered under method "reference_only_adaptation" because five occurrences of two
    cross-references pointing outside the excerpt were adapted as set out in point 2; every adapted occurrence
    and its replacement is recorded in delivery-evidence.json and no other character of the span was changed;
  * the authors' own numbering and macro declarations, copied verbatim from source0.tex lines 18-27
    (\numberwithin for equations, the section-based shared theorem counter, and the plain/definition/remark
    theorem styles) and lines 39-49 (the operators \argmax, \argmin and \KL and the macros \R, \1, \ip, \norm,
    \Hent, \simplex, \softmax and \avgscore), re-declared in the wrapper preamble because the wrapper is an
    amsart document rather than the article-class document of the Work;
  * the authors' own bibliography, transcribed verbatim from the article's own BibTeX output CD_LLM.bbl shipped
    in the e-print: all 17 entries with their natbib author-year data and their published numbers [1]-[17];
  * the authors' own citation commands (\citet, \citep, \citealp) untouched, and natbib loaded with the
    article's own options [sort&compress,numbers], so that every printed citation of the delivered excerpt is
    rendered exactly as in the version of record (for example "Cover and Thomas [7]", "Jaynes [10]",
    "[2, 3, 13]", "Boyd and Vandenberghe [5, Section 3.1.4]" and the author's own "5, Ch. 3" slip at source
    line 177);
  * the authors' own numbering, so that every printed section, subsection, theorem, proposition, lemma,
    corollary, definition, remark and equation number of the delivered unit coincides with the published version
    (the counted statement is printed as Theorem 4.1 and its vector field as equation (4.1), as in the version of
    record), including the author's own "(Proof (sketch))" heading printed for Proposition 3.7.

2. EDITORIAL ADDITIONS AND BOUNDED ADAPTATIONS (NOT AUTHOR MATERIAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus, together
    with the bbm package needed for the indicator macro \mathbbm{1} and the natbib package needed for the
    author's citation commands;
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered item;
  * five occurrences of two cross-references that point outside the delivered excerpt - three references at
    source lines 104, 151 and 158 to Section 6, and two references at source lines 157 and 269 to Section 5 -
    are delivered as hyperlinks to the version PDF carrying the published numbers 6 and 5; the printed text of
    the delivered excerpt is unchanged by this adaptation;
  * the article's own 17-entry bibliography is carried in the wrapper text (after the counted proof) rather than
    through the corpus driver's bibitem mechanism, because that mechanism is keyed to plain \cite commands
    while this article cites exclusively through natbib's \citet, \citep and \citealp; the bibliography text
    itself is the article's own .bbl file, byte-for-byte, so nothing about the reference list is editorial except
    its placement.

These additions are formatting, provenance and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical statement, proof
step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or altered, and the
printed slips of the Work are kept literal.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK
---------------------------------------
The Work's two PNG figures (softmax_bundle_1.png and softmax_bundle_2.png) lie outside the delivered excerpt, in
the excluded Sections 5-6, and no figure, diagram or artwork of any kind is included in this package: the
delivered range contains no \includegraphics command. The Work's publisher class file is not used, not shipped
and not redistributed: the Work is itself an article-class manuscript and the wrapper is an editorial AMS
formatting layer.
