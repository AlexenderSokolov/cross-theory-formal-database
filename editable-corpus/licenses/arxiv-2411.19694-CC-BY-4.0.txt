WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/169340_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Lemma 6.4 (native source lines 712-714, printed on page 14: the
  Hopf-type Strong Comparison Principle for the singular mixed local-nonlocal operator when s lies in
  [1/2,1)) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines 716-765, one proof environment of 50
  lines, adjacent to the statement); and verbatim the source-local context that proof needs, namely the
  statement of the linear Strong Comparison Principle with its displayed system (lines 174-184, printed as
  Theorem 1.3), the statement of Lemma 6.1 with the displayed differential inequality it produces (lines
  611-613 and 621-623), the statement of the extension lemma with its equation for e_0 (lines 650-659,
  printed as Lemma 6.2), the statement of the companion lemma for s in (0,1/2) (lines 682-684, printed as
  Lemma 6.3) and the article's own complete proof of that companion lemma (lines 686-710), which defines the
  auxiliary functions w and v used by the counted proof. Every retained line is the article's own line,
  unchanged, apart from the declared adaptation recorded next.
* Declared adaptation, and the only difference from the source lines: the two attribution commands in the
  retained proof of Lemma 6.3 are each replaced, one for one and with nothing else changed, by a DOI
  hyperlink carrying the same bibliographic details transcribed from the article's own bibliography file in
  the e-print: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2025.01.030 for the work of C. A. Antonini and M. Cozzi on
  global gradient regularity and a Hopf lemma for quasilinear operators of mixed local-nonlocal type, and
  https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.20153 for the work of L. Silvestre on regularity of the obstacle problem for a
  fractional power of the Laplace operator. Both substitutions are listed in this item's reference mapping
  and in the package delivery evidence. Nothing was omitted from any retained span, so no omission receipt
  exists for this package and no other adaptation is declared anywhere in it.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper keeps the article's theorem-like environments on one shared counter
  but without the article's section-based prefix, so the retained items print as Theorem 1, Lemma 2, Lemma
  3, Lemma 4 and Lemma 5, whereas the article prints the same items as Theorem 1.3, Lemma 6.1, Lemma 6.2,
  Lemma 6.3 and Lemma 6.4; the fourteen numbered displays of the retained lines print as (1) to (14) in
  order of appearance, whereas the article numbers its equations per section.
* sources/169340/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file three_sol_arxiv.tex except that the
  pipeline's mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added,
  removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only; the article itself is typeset with the AMS amsart class.
