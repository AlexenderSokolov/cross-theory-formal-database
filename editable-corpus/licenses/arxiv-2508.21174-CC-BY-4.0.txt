1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one theorem of the Work, delivered with its complete original
proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the authors' own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex at the
    exact line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json (method "exact_tex": source lines 42-237, 451-456 and
    457-459, the last two being the counted Theorem 2 and its complete proof);
  * the authors' own context text reproduced verbatim at source lines 238-292, 293-352 and 353-450, with only
    the bounded reference adaptations set out in point 2 below (method "reference_only_adaptation", the adapted
    spans and every replacement being recorded in delivery-evidence.json);
  * the authors' own theorem declarations, copied verbatim from source0.tex lines 19-24 (\newtheorem for lemma,
    proposition, theorem, corollary, remark and definition), re-declared in the wrapper preamble because the
    wrapper is an amsart document rather than the article-class document of the Work;
  * the authors' own bibliography entries, transcribed verbatim from the article's own BibTeX output
    TEhomog-FC-SM.bbl shipped in the e-print, 27 of its 28 entries, each cited with the article's printed
    bibliography number ([1]-[17], [19]-[28]; the uncited entry [18] is omitted);
  * the authors' own numbering, so that every printed part, section, subsection, theorem, lemma, proposition,
    corollary, remark and equation number of the delivered unit coincides with the published version (the
    counted statement is printed as Theorem 2 and its displayed formula as equation (84), as in the version of
    record).

2. EDITORIAL ADDITIONS AND BOUNDED ADAPTATIONS (NOT AUTHOR MATERIAL)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus;
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered item;
  * a one-line editorial declaration of \newblock and the wrapper's theorem declarations, which only restate
    the authors' own preamble for the amsart wrapper and change no printed content;
  * four occurrences of three cross-references that point outside the delivered excerpt - the two references at
    source line 413 to the appendix subsection on the derivative of T_0 and to the appendix equation (89)
    defining the solution map L_tau, and the two references at source lines 332 and 339 to the appendix trace
    theorem - are delivered as hyperlinks to the version PDF carrying the published numbers 7.1, 89 and 7.2;
    the printed text of the delivered excerpt is unchanged by this adaptation;
  * five equation labels that the authors declare twice in the source (lines 238, 293, 295, 353 and 355, namely
    eq:uepssystem, fourthordeqest, homogeqest, fourthordeqest2 and homogeqest2) carry an editorial suffix in
    their second declaration so that the delivered document has no multiply-defined labels. None of those five
    names is referenced anywhere in the Work, labels are not printed, and no printed number, formula, sentence,
    hypothesis or proof step is affected.

These additions are formatting, provenance and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical statement, proof
step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or altered, and the
printed slips of the Work are kept literal.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK
---------------------------------------
The Work contains no figures, no diagrams and no artwork of any kind, and none is included in this package; no
\includegraphics command occurs in the delivered range. The Work's publisher class file is not used, not shipped
and not redistributed: the Work is itself an article-class manuscript and the wrapper is an editorial AMS
formatting layer.
