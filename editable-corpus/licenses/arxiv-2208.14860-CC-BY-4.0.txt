WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/8067_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces,
  verbatim and in the article's own notation, the article's Lemma 7.7 (finding a
  cycle with exactly k chords from a large collection of pairwise vertex-disjoint
  induced copies of K_{l,l} with no edges between them, native source lines
  1189-1191) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines 1193-1372),
  and verbatim excerpts of the statements of the lemmas it uses (the twenty-
  squares lemma, lines 660-662; the skewed-squares lemma, lines 688-690; the two
  lemmas bounding edges from induced K_{l,l} copies to a unimodal path, lines
  763-766 and 796-800; the cleaning-step lemma, lines 995-1004; the induced-
  matching lemma, lines 1038-1041; and the case-analysis lemma with its six
  labelled cases, lines 1048-1074), of the observation on unimodal paths in
  different layers (lines 293-296), and of the subsection heading and
  introductory paragraph of the counted lemma (lines 1184-1187).
* sources/8067/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as
  distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the
  e-print file chi.tex except that the pipeline's mechanical concatenation
  prepends one header comment line naming the file it was read from
  ("% concatenated from chi.tex"); nothing else is added, removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages (with enumitem and
  cleveref for the article's labelled enumerations and cross-references), and
  this notice. These are editorial formatting only.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No figure is delivered as an image. The counted proof displays three auxiliary
pictures in three figure environments; the package reproduces those three
environments with their placement, captions and labels but without their images,
so exactly three lines of the retained proof (the three includegraphics commands,
naming figures/lem23a.pdf, figures/lem23b.pdf and figures/lem23c.pdf) are
omitted, no other line is changed, and the package declares no asset dependency.
No bibliography and no bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation.
No publisher class or style file, no upstream script, no font and no unrelated
artwork from any other source.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or
re-derived, and the wrapper adds only the editorial material described in the
document's own scope paragraph.
