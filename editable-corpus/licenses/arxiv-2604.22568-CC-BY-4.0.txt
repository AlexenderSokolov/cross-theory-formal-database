EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The wrapper (amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is

as Corollary 2, the retained Schur-complement lemma as Lemma 4, the counted
statement as Theorem 2, and the numbered displays of the retained spans carry the
paper's own equation numbers. That formatting difference is editorial and alters
