1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one theorem or proposition of the Work, delivered with its
complete original proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the authors' own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex at the exact line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json (method "exact_tex");
  * the eight occurrences of forward cross-references at source0.tex lines 287, 289, 291, 293, 302 and 821, delivered as \href links to the version PDF with the printed numbers retained (method "reference_only_adaptation", the adapted lines are set out in delivery-evidence.json);
  * the authors' own macro block, \newtheorem declarations and \numberwithin settings, copied verbatim;
  * the authors' own bibliography entries, transcribed verbatim from the article's own inline thebibliography at source0.tex lines 4199-4303 with the article's printed bibliography numbers;
  * the authors' own numbering, so that every printed part, section, subsection, theorem, lemma, claim, proposition, corollary, definition, example, remark and equation number of the delivered unit is produced by the authors' own counters (the counted theorem is printed as Theorem 4.11, as in the published version);

2. EDITORIAL ADDITIONS (NOT AUTHOR MATERIAL)
--------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus;
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered item;
  * a one-line editorial shim that makes the article's \index commands no-ops, because the article's own makeidx package is commented out in its preamble while its \makeindex call is not, and the article never prints an index; the printed page content is unchanged;

These additions are formatting, provenance and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical statement, proof
step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or altered, and the
printed slips of the Work are kept literal.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK
---------------------------------------
The Work contains no figures, no diagrams and no artwork of any kind, and none is included in this package. The Work's publisher class file is not used, not shipped and not redistributed: the Work is itself an amsart article.
