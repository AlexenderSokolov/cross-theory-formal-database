WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/192333_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's lemma X (native source lines 850-867) together with the article's
  complete proof of it (lines 869-1085, one proof environment of 217 lines). Retained uncounted as the source-local context the proof needs: lines 248-250; lines 336-343; lines 371-384; lines 396-408; lines 475-492; lines 585-600; lines 589-591; lines 602-615; lines 611-613; lines 660-662; lines 807-813; lines 827-844; lines 841-842.
  One declared adaptation: the 3 image-inclusion commands inside the retained spans are omitted, so exactly 3 lines of the retained spans differ from the source and no other line does; the figure environments themselves, with their placement, captions and labels, are kept, and the package records the omitted strings in the fidelity receipts bound to the affected bodies. No image is delivered and the package declares no asset dependency.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper declares the retained theorem-like environments on separate counters, so the retained items print as Proposition 1, Hypothesis 1, Definition 1, Lemma 1 in order of appearance, whereas the article prints the same items with the numbering of its own full document; the numbered displays of the retained lines print consecutively in order of appearance, whereas the article numbers its equations per section.
* Bibliography: the retained lines cite 3 works, whose entries are transcribed verbatim from the article's own bibliography source in the e-print and delivered with short editorial labels; the mapping is recorded in the item's reference mapping. No other bibliography entry is transcribed and no retained line cites anything else.
* No image and no artwork: no retained line contains a figure environment or an image-inclusion command, and the retained text contains no TikZ or drawing code; the package declares no asset dependency.
* sources/192333/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is the article's own source as the pipeline concatenated it, byte identical to the e-print files apart from the single header comment line naming them; nothing else is added, removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting only.
