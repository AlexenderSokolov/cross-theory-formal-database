WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
--------------------------
This package reproduces, under the CC BY 4.0 licence above, text that the three
named authors wrote and that arXiv distributes for this version:

  * items/200710_author_proof.tex - an editorial amsart assembly of the paper's
    own LaTeX source lines. The counted statement (source0.tex lines 1185-1193)
    and the counted proof (source0.tex lines 1195-1279) are the authors' text
    verbatim, and so is every retained context span (source0.tex lines 1089-1097
    and 1109-1113). Nothing was translated, corrected, retypeset by hand or
    re-derived. Line-by-line binding of every retained span against
    cached-originals/source0.tex is carried in delivery-evidence.json.
  * sources/200710/source0.tex - the prep lane's mechanical concatenation of the
    paper's own single .tex source, byte-identical to
    cached-originals/source0.tex.
  * manifest.json, delivery-evidence.json, INDEX.md, provenance.json, schema.sql
    and the rebuild/restore/test scripts are this lane's own metadata and
    tooling.

ASSETS, GRAPHICS AND INCLUDES
-----------------------------
The paper draws its illustrations inline with its own drawing code rather than
with image files, and the illustration that belongs to the counted proof is
reproduced in exactly that form, as the authors wrote it; the paper's drawing
libraries, its styles and its drawing macros were restated in the wrapper so that
it compiles. No retained span contains a graphics-inclusion command, no image,
plot or artwork file exists in or is redistributed with this package, and the
manifest and the delivery evidence both carry an empty asset-dependency list. The
paper's own source uses no input or include directive for a separate .tex file:

SOURCE_MAPPING.json.

EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is the standard LaTeX article class, which is not a
publisher class, and that class is not shipped with this package. The wrapper
(amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is this lane's
editorial formatting. It restates the paper's own theorem-like declarations with
their shared section-relative counter, its own drawing libraries and its own
shorthand macros, all copied verbatim, and it restores the paper's shared
theorem counter before the retained material, so that the printed numbers of the
delivered material are the paper's own: the retained bipartite triangle lemma
prints as Lemma 3.2, the retained disjoint-paths lemma as Lemma 3.4 and the
counted statement as Lemma 3.5. Those formatting differences are editorial and
alter no formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
