EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The wrapper (amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is
this lane's editorial formatting. It restores the paper's separate per-kind
theorem counters, its appendix lemma numbering and its global equation counter
before the retained material, so that the printed numbers of the delivered
material are the paper's own: the retained Schwarzschild theorem prints as
Theorem 2, the counted statement as Corollary 1, the retained approximants lemma
as Lemma B.3, and the numbered displays of the retained spans carry the paper's
own equation numbers (13), (23), (33) and (34). That formatting difference is
editorial and alters no formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
