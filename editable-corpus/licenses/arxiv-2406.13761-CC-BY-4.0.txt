WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/161045_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Theorem 2 (native source lines 457-473, the global convergence
  bound C h^p for the METD_p scheme) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines 475-612, one
  pf environment of 138 lines, adjacent to the statement), and verbatim the source-local context that proof
  needs: the model problem (lines 71-75, printed as equation (1) in the article); the truncated local term
  I_p and the METD_p update (lines 271-282, printed as equations (19) and (20) in the article); the
  Banach-algebra definition and the two standing assumptions (lines 329-338 and 345-348, printed as
  Definition 1, Assumption 1 and Assumption 2); and the statement of the article's local defect theorem
  (lines 359-366, printed as Theorem 1), whose own proof is not retained because the counted proof invokes
  it as a black box. Every retained line is the article's own line, unchanged; nothing is omitted, so the
  package ships no omission record. The wrapper numbers theorem-like environments exactly as the article
  does (per type, not per section), but it numbers displayed equations consecutively, so the five retained
  displayed items print in this document as (1) to (5) whereas the article prints them as (1), (19), (20),
  (27) and (28).
* sources/161045/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file main.tex except that the pipeline's
  mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added, removed or
  changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image and no artwork. No retained line contains a figure environment, an image-inclusion command or any
drawing code, and the package declares no asset dependency. No bibliography and no bibliography entry: no
retained line contains a citation. No publisher class or style file: the article was typeset with the
standard article class and the e-print ships no class or style file of its own. No upstream script, no font
and no unrelated artwork from any other source. No content was deleted from any retained span.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
