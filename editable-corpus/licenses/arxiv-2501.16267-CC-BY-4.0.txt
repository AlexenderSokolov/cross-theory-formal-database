WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/172262_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Theorem 1.4 (native source lines 129-159, printed on page 2: the del
  Pezzo surface of degree 2 over Q(sqrt(-7)) that is smooth, has automorphism group PSL_2(F_7) x Z/2Z and
  Picard group Z, and has no rational points) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines
  415-500, one proof environment of 86 lines carrying the article's own optional title); and verbatim the
  statements, and only the statements, of the three results that proof invokes, namely the completion lemma
  (lines 205-214, printed as Lemma 2.3), the corollary reducing the equation modulo 64 (lines 326-332,
  printed as Corollary 2.6) and the centralizer lemma (lines 355-357, printed as Lemma 2.7). Every retained
  line is the article's own line, unchanged; nothing was omitted from any retained span, so the package
  carries no omission record and declares no adaptation.
* A two-entry bibliography. The retained lines cite exactly two works, and their entries are transcribed
  verbatim from the article's own bibliography block in the bundled source (its entries on lines 584 and
  588-590), with the short editorial labels Dol12 and Elk01; the transcription is recorded in the item's
  reference mapping. No other bibliography entry is transcribed, and no retained line cites anything else.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper keeps the article's theorem-like environments on one shared counter
  but without the article's subsection-based prefix, so the retained items print as Lemma 1, Corollary 2,
  Lemma 3 and Theorem 4 in order of appearance, whereas the article prints the same four items as Lemma 2.3,
  Corollary 2.6, Lemma 2.7 and Theorem 1.4; the displayed equations of the retained lines print as (1) to
  (5), whereas the article numbers its equations per section.
* sources/172262/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file Brauer-ManindP2.tex except that the
  pipeline's mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added,
  removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only; the article itself is typeset with the AMS amsart class.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image and no artwork: no retained line contains a figure environment, an image-inclusion command or any
drawing code, and the package declares no asset dependency. No publisher class or style file, no upstream
script, no font and no unrelated artwork from any other source. No content was deleted from any retained
span apart from nothing at all: every retained line is the article's own line, unchanged.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
