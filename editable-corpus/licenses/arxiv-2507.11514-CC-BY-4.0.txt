1. WHAT IS RETAINED IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------------------
This package is an editable LaTeX projection of one proposition of the Work, delivered with its complete
original proof and with the source-local context required to read it. It retains:

  * the author's own LaTeX source text, reproduced verbatim from the e-print source file source0.tex at the
    exact line spans recorded in delivery-evidence.json (method "exact_tex" for all five bodies), namely source
    lines 87--331 (the whole Introduction and the first part of Section 2 up to the counted statement),
    332--338 (the counted proposition), 339--652 (the remainder of Section 2, the whole of Section 3 with the
    proof of the article's main theorem, and the whole of Section 4 with the geometric results), 654--723 (the
    complete proof of the counted proposition, that is the whole of Section 5 exactly as printed, containing the
    auxiliary geometric Lemma 5.1 with its proof) and 724--873 (the whole of Sections 6 and 7 with the remaining
    proofs);
  * the author's own title, author, address, e-mail and funding thanks lines and abstract, reproduced verbatim
    from source lines 70--73, 76 and 81--83;
  * the author's own overline and underline aliases, \newtheorem block and macro block, copied verbatim from
    source lines 11--16, 20--31 and 33--65;
  * the author's own bibliography entries for all 14 works cited by the delivered text, transcribed verbatim
    from the e-print's own bibliography file arxiv-new.bbl in that file's order, which is the article's printed
    bibliography order, with the article's printed bibliography numbers;
  * the author's own numbering, so that every printed section, subsection, definition, theorem, corollary,
    claim, proposition, lemma, equation and citation number of the delivered unit coincides with the published
    version (the counted proposition is printed as Proposition 2.10, as in the published version).

--------------------------------------------
  * the AMS amsart wrapper preamble and page geometry supplied by the pinned runtime of this corpus, together
    with the one package this unit needs beyond it (enumitem);
  * the replacement of the article's BibTeX calls by the transcription of the article's own bibliography file
    described in section 1; the two bibliography command lines at source lines 874--875 and the closing
    document marker are therefore not part of any delivered span;
  * the title block, the source-and-licence line and the editorial scope note at the head of the delivered
    item;

These additions are formatting, provenance and runtime-compatibility only. No mathematical statement, proof
step, symbol, hypothesis or number of the Work has been written, repaired, re-derived or altered, and the
printed slips of the Work are kept literal, including the author's two unused note macros and the stray closing
parenthesis inside an exponent at source line 860.

3. NO PUBLISHER MATERIAL AND NO ARTWORK
---------------------------------------
The Work contains no figure, no table, no diagram and no artwork of any kind, and none is included in this
package; the e-print source contains no \includegraphics and no \input. The Work is itself an amsart article,
so no publisher class file, publisher typesetting, upstream script or font of a publisher is used, shipped or
redistributed, and no part of a publisher PDF is redistributed. The bibliography transcription redistributes
only the author's own reference list as it appears in the e-print's own bibliography file.
