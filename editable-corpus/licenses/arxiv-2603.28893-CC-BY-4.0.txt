--------------------------

author wrote and that arXiv distributes for this version:

EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is the standard LaTeX article class with the a4paper
option, which is not a publisher class, and that class is not shipped with this
package. The wrapper (amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without
bookmarks) is this lane's editorial formatting. It restates the paper's own
theorem-like declarations with their shared section-relative counter, its own
cleveref names, its own QED mark and example style, and its whole notation block
(the alphabet generator, the operator declarations and the shorthand macros used
by the retained spans), all copied verbatim, so that the printed numbers of the
delivered material are the paper's own: the retained section prints as
Section 5, the retained assumption as Assumption 1, the counted statement as
Proposition 5.4, and the numbered displays of the proof as (5.5) and (5.6). Two
editorial counter settings restore the paper's section, proposition and equation
numbers before the retained material. Those formatting differences are editorial
and alter no formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
