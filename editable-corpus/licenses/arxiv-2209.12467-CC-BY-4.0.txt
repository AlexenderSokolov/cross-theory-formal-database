WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/8068_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces,
  verbatim and in the article's own notation, the article's Proposition 2 (its
  convergence-rate proposition for Markov chains with conditions C1, C2, C3 and
  conclusions (I) and (II), native source lines 455-475) together with the
  article's complete proof of it (lines 2410-2425, the appendix subsection
  "Proof of Proposition 2"), and verbatim excerpts of the article's Erratum
  notice (lines 178-181), its abstract (lines 186-192), its informal definition
  of linear convergence with rate r (lines 426-429), its sentences introducing
  the proposition and its appendix proof (lines 451-454) and its appendix
  section heading (line 2400). The retained text cites exactly one work, and the
  wrapper reproduces that one entry of the article's own bibliography, with the
  article's printed number as its label: [7] Yuan Shih Chow et al., On a strong
  law of large numbers for martingales, The Annals of Mathematical Statistics
  38(2):610-610, 1967.
* sources/8068/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as
  distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the
  e-print file template.tex except that the pipeline's mechanical concatenation
  prepends one header comment line naming the file it was read from
  ("% concatenated from template.tex"); nothing else is added, removed or
  changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages (including xcolor for the
  blue correction marks and cleveref for the cross-references), and this notice.
  These are editorial formatting only.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No figure, diagram, photograph or plot: the article's 22 graphics inclusions
(its author photographs and the plots of its numerical evaluation) lie outside
every retained span, the retained lines contain no graphics-inclusion command,
and the package declares no asset dependency. No bibliography beyond the single
cited entry reproduced in the wrapper. No publisher class or style file: the
article's IEEEtran class is neither shipped nor executed, and the wrapper is an
AMS amsart document with its own fonts. No upstream script, no font and no
unrelated artwork from any other source.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or
re-derived, and the wrapper adds only the editorial material described in the
document's own scope paragraph.
