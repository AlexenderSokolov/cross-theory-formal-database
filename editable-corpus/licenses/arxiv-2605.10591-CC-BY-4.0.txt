EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is the standard AMS amsart class with the 12pt and
a4paper options; that class is not a publisher class, and the wrapper loads the
same AMS class with this lane's own editorial formatting (10pt, A4, geometric
margins, hyperref without bookmarks). The package restates the paper's own
theorem-like declarations with their shared section-relative counter and its own
shorthand macros, all copied verbatim, and it restores the paper's global
equation counter and its section-relative theorem counter before the retained
material, so that the printed numbers of the delivered material are the paper's
own: the retained displays carry the paper's equation numbers (1), (2) and (5),
and the counted statement prints as Proposition 3.13. Those formatting
differences are editorial and alter no formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
