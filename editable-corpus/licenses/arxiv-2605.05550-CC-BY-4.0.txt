EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The wrapper (amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is
this lane's editorial formatting. The package restates the paper's own
theorem-like declarations with their shared section-relative counter, its own
shorthand macros and the small support packages its retained text needs, all
taken from the paper's own source, and it restores the paper's section and
theorem counters before each retained group, so that the printed numbers of the
delivered material are the paper's own: the retained contraction theorem prints
as Theorem 2.1, the retained tree-expansion corollary as Corollary 2.2 and the
counted statement as Lemma 5.2. Those formatting differences are editorial and
alter no formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
