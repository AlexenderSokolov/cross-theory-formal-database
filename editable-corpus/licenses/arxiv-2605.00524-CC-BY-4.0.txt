EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is the standard LaTeX article class at 10pt; that class is
not a publisher class, and the wrapper loads the AMS amsart class with this
lane's own editorial formatting (10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without
bookmarks). The package restates the paper's own theorem-like declarations with
their shared section-relative counter and the shorthand macros its retained text
uses, all copied verbatim, and it restores the paper's section and theorem
counters before the counted statement, so that the printed number of the
delivered statement is the paper's own: Theorem 3.2. That formatting difference
is editorial and alters no formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
