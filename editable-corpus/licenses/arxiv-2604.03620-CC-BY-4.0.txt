EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is amsart with the reqno option and a custom paper size of
192mm by 262mm. That class is the standard AMS class, not a publisher class, and
the wrapper loads the same AMS class; the wrapper's own formatting (10pt, A4,
geometric margins, hyperref without bookmarks) is this lane's editorial
formatting. The package restates the paper's own theorem-like declarations with
their shared global counter and its own mathematical shorthand macros, all
copied verbatim from the paper's preamble, and it restores the paper's global
theorem and equation counters before each retained group, so that the printed
numbers of the delivered material are the paper's own: the retained corollary
prints as Corollary 8, the retained unstratified theorem as Theorem 11, the
counted statement as Theorem 13, and the numbered displays of the retained spans
carry the paper's own equation numbers. The paper suppresses the printed numbers
of displays its text does not reference; the wrapper leaves them printed, so
unreferenced displays additionally show the same numbers the paper assigns them.
Those formatting differences are editorial and alter no formula, symbol,
hypothesis or argument.
