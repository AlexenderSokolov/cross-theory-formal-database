* items/133760_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's prop prop (native source lines 2389-2390) together with the article's
  complete proof of it (lines 2392-2453, one proof environment of 62 lines). Retained uncounted as the source-local context the proof needs: lines 2383-2386.
  One declared adaptation: the 2 image-inclusion commands inside the retained spans are omitted, so exactly 2 lines of the retained spans differ from the source and no other line does; the figure environments themselves, with their placement, captions and labels, are kept, and the package records the omitted strings in the fidelity receipts bound to the affected bodies. No image is delivered and the package declares no asset dependency.
* Numbering as delivered: the wrapper declares the retained theorem-like environments on separate counters, so the retained items print as Proposition 1 in order of appearance, whereas the article prints the same items with the numbering of its own full document; the numbered displays of the retained lines print consecutively in order of appearance, whereas the article numbers its equations per section.
* No bibliography and no bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation.
