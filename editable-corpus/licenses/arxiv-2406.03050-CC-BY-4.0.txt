WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/160309_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Theorem 4.14 (native source lines 977-980) together with the
  article's complete proof of it (lines 981-985, one proof environment, adjacent to the statement), and
  verbatim the source-local context that proof needs: the contiguous run of lines 916-936, which carries
  the [P]-torsion identifications, the article's definition of the Sigma_0-imprimitive signed Selmer group
  (printed as Definition 4.10), the isomorphism it induces (printed as equation (19)) and the statement of
  the cotorsion lemma the proof invokes (printed as Lemma 4.11); and the statement of the article's
  Proposition 4.13 (lines 948-951), without its proof. Every retained line is the article's own line,
  unchanged; nothing is omitted, so the package ships no omission record.
* sources/160309/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the
  arXiv e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file revisionIJNT2.tex with the
  article's own macro file canonicalheader.tex spliced in at the point where the article reads it, except
  that the pipeline's mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming the spliced files;
  nothing else is added, removed or changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only.
