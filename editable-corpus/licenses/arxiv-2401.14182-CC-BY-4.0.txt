Components delivered by this package. Verbatim source lines of the article for the counted statement, for the counted proof, and for the eight uncounted context passages named in the item's editorial scope paragraph, copied line for line from the article's own TeX source and bundled as sources/153512/source0.tex; the delivered item items/153512_author_proof.tex; and this notice. An editorial AMS amsart wrapper supplies formatting, the article's theorem-environment declarations, three of its macros and its labelled-enumeration package.

Components omitted. The remainder of the article, including its other sections, its remaining propositions, lemmas and theorems, and its figures; the article's bibliography, since no retained line carries a citation and no reference entry is delivered; and all artwork. No retained span contains any picture, diagram or graphical inclusion, and no image asset is delivered or redistributed. The article's own class and style files, its fonts, its bibliography databases and any other publisher or upstream file are neither shipped nor redistributed, and no such file is present in this package. One editorial font difference is declared: the article's Euler script font for its script letters is replaced by the wrapper's own script font, and no symbol changes meaning.

Retention method. Every retained span is recorded in delivery-evidence.json with method exact_tex; no retained line is altered, re-flowed or partially omitted, and no bounded-source-comparison body exists.
