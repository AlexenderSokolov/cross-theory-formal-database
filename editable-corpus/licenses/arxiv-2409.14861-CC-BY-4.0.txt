WHAT THIS PACKAGE CONTAINS
* items/165656_author_proof.tex - one independent LaTeX document that reproduces, verbatim and in the
  article's own notation, the article's Theorem 2 (native source lines 357-358: the free/forgetful pair,
  the unit and the natural transformation built from the E-operators form an adjunction whose induced monad
  is the Giry monad) together with the article's complete proof of it (lines 359-428, one proof environment
  of 70 lines, adjacent to the statement); the source-local context that proof needs, namely the
  affine-measurable-function lemma with the article's own proof of it (lines 307-316, printed in the article
  as Lemma 5) and the paragraph and lemma introducing the free functor, the forgetful functor and the
  natural transformation E (lines 340-355, printed in the article as Lemma 7 and the sentence following its
  proof); and the diagram code the proof itself displays, carried with the node and path commands exactly as
  the article wrote them, apart from the declared adaptation recorded in the next paragraph. The wrapper
  numbers its retained theorem-like environments consecutively per type, so it prints the retained
  affine-measurable-function lemma as Lemma 1, the retained natural-transformation lemma as Lemma 2 and the
  counted theorem as Theorem 1, whereas the article prints the same three items as Lemma 5, Lemma 7 and
  Theorem 2; the article's own textual mentions of its numbers inside the retained proof are kept literally.
* Declared adaptation, and the only difference from the source lines: the stray option string {scale=5},
  which the article attaches twice to a diagram environment inside the counted proof although that
  environment accepts only a bracketed option, is omitted; the article's own typesetting already drops that
  string, every character of it being typeset in no font. The package records this omitted string in the
  fidelity receipt bound to the delivered proof, whose role is exactly the counted proof, and declares no
  other adaptation anywhere else in the package.
* sources/165656/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv
  e-print for this version. It is byte identical to the e-print file kirk.tex except that the pipeline's
  mechanical concatenation prepends one header comment line naming it; nothing else is added, removed or
  changed.
* an AMS amsart wrapper, its standard TeX Live packages, and this notice. These are editorial formatting
  only. The wrapper loads the standard tikz package that the article itself uses for its diagrams.

WHAT THIS PACKAGE DOES NOT CONTAIN
No image file and no external artwork: the retained spans contain no figure environment and no
image-inclusion command, all drawing material being the article's own TikZ code kept inside the delivered
source text. No bibliography and no bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation. No publisher
class or style file, no upstream script, no font and no unrelated artwork from any other source. Apart from
the declared adaptation recorded above, no content was deleted from any retained span.

The mathematical text is unchanged: no mathematics is written, repaired or re-derived, and the wrapper adds
only the editorial material described in the document's own scope paragraph.
