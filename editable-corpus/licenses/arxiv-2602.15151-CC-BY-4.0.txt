EDITORIAL WRAPPER
-----------------
The paper's own class is the standard LaTeX article class with the letterpaper
option, which is not a publisher class, and that class is not shipped with this
package. The wrapper (amsart, 10pt, A4, geometric margins, hyperref without
bookmarks) is this lane's editorial formatting. It restates the paper's own nine
theorem-like declarations with their shared section-relative counter, and its
global equation counter, so that the printed numbers of the delivered statements
are the paper's own: Section 2, Observation 2.1, Theorem 2.5 and the displayed
formulas (4) and (5). That formatting difference is editorial and alters no
formula, symbol, hypothesis or argument.
