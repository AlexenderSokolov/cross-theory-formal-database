  article's own notation, the article's proposition ValidAreBoolean (native source lines 1182-1184) together with the article's
  complete proof of it (lines 1185-1234, one proof environment of 50 lines). Retained uncounted as the source-local context the proof needs: lines 150-152; lines 663-665; lines 779-785; lines 878-880; lines 891-897; lines 936-938; lines 954-956; lines 960-962; lines 1096-1098; lines 1138-1140; lines 1143-1145; lines 1150-1153; lines 1170-1175.

* No bibliography and no bibliography entry: no retained line contains a citation.
* No image and no artwork: no retained line contains a figure environment or an image-inclusion command, and the retained text contains no TikZ or drawing code; the package declares no asset dependency.
* sources/208689/source0.tex - the complete native author source of the article as distributed in the arXiv e-print for this version. It is the article's own source as the pipeline concatenated it, byte identical to the e-print files apart from the single header comment line naming them; nothing else is added, removed or changed.
