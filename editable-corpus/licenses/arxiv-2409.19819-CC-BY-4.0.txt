Components delivered by this package. An editorial AMS amsart wrapper supplies formatting, the article's theorem-environment declarations and its macros, together with the xy and comment packages that the article itself uses.

Components omitted. The remainder of the article, including its main theorems, its Euler-system construction and its other propositions; the article's bibliography, since no retained line carries a citation and no reference entry is delivered; and all artwork. No retained span contains any picture, diagram or graphical inclusion, and no image asset is delivered or redistributed; the commutative diagrams of the retained spans are drawn by the retained diagram code itself. The article's own class and style files, its fonts, its bibliography databases and any other publisher or upstream file are neither shipped nor redistributed, and no such file is present in this package.

Retention method. Every retained span is recorded in delivery-evidence.json with method exact_tex; no retained line is altered, re-flowed or partially omitted, and no bounded-source-comparison body exists.
