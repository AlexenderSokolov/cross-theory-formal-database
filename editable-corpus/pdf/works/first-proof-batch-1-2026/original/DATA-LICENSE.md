# Data license & provenance

This file records who produced the material in this repository and on what basis
First Proof publishes it. `NOTICE` states the licenses; this file states the
provenance behind them.

The governing principle: **First Proof licenses only what First Proof produced or
holds the rights to.** Nothing here is a blanket grant over third-party work.

## Covered by CC-BY-SA-4.0 (see `LICENSE-DOCS`)

**Problem statements** (`First_Proof.tex`). Written and curated for the First Proof
project, and released with the batch-1 paper (arXiv:2602.05192).

**Author solutions and commentary** (`author-solutions/`,
`FirstProofSolutionsComments.pdf`). Written for the First Proof project as the
verified human baseline for the ten problems, and published by First Proof as part
of it.

**AI-generated answers** (`FirstProof-Gemini3Pro-answers/`,
`FirstProof-GPT5.2Pro-answers/`, and the AI logs in the supplementary archive).
These are the outputs of runs First Proof commissioned, configured and executed
against the batch-1 problems. They are published as run artifacts of the benchmark.

**Documentation.** This file, `NOTICE`, and `README.md`.

## Reserved: AGPL-3.0

There is no source code in this repository. The `LICENSE` file carries the
AGPL-3.0 text so that any code added later is covered on the same terms First
Proof uses elsewhere.

## Third-party material

This repository contains no third-party submitted codebase. (Batch 2 does — see
that repository's `DATA-LICENSE.md`, where the three submitters' codebases are
explicitly excluded from First Proof's grants.)

Any later contribution that originates with another author is **not** swept under
the grants above. It is published only with permission, licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0
where First Proof holds the rights, and otherwise marked "© respective authors,
published with permission." Where the rights status of any material is unclear, it
is flagged and withheld rather than published.

## Relationship to First Proof's DARPA agreement

This batch was released in February 2026, well **before** DARPA agreement
HR0011263E126 took effect (21 July 2026), and was not generated under it. The
Government's Unlimited Rights under that agreement attach to data developed or
generated under the agreement and therefore do not reach this material. The
licenses above are First Proof's own grants to the public and are unaffected by it.
