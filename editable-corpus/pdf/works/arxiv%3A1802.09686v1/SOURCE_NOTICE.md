## Editorial source note on physical page 4

Formula (8) can fix a non-packed monomial before alternation. For example, the monomial x1*x2^2 for alpha=(3) has shifted exponents (3,3,0). In the sum (7), such repeated shifted exponents give equal columns in the author alternant on physical pages 1–2 and contribute zero over a commutative ring. The packed monomial is the only possible surviving fixed contribution. The original printed wording and proof are retained as supplied.
