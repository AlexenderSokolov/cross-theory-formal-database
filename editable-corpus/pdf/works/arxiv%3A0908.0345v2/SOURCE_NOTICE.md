# A Pieri rule for skew shapes

Sami H. Assaf and Peter R. W. McNamara give the selected positive-degree result. Thomas Lam authored the appendix.

Richard P. Stanley posed [Exercise 44](../../../tex/works/stanley%252Fch7-supplement-20230604-solutions-20230324/original/ch7supp.pdf#page=10) and supplied its [source pointer](../../../tex/works/stanley%252Fch7-supplement-20230604-solutions-20230324/original/ch7suppsol.pdf#page=17). The original exercise uses k ≥ 0. The counted author proof is Theorem 3.2 for positive degree; the degree-zero boundary follows from the empty-strip convention.
