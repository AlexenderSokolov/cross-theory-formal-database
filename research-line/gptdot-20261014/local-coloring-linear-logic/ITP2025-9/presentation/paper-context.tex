\section*{Author main-result context}
\textbf{Theorem 1 (Revisited; references [35], [25]).}
Let $G$ be one of three specific graded unipotent Chevalley groups ($A_{3}$, $B_{3}$-small, or $B_{3}$-large),
and let $\Phi$ be a positive root system for $G$.
Then the full set of Steinberg relations $R_{\text{full}}$ for $G$ can be derived from the weak relations $R_{\text{weak}}$.
In other words,
the presentations $\langle S\mid R_{\text{full}}\rangle$ and $\langle S\mid R_{\text{weak}}\rangle$ are isomorphic.
\par\medskip
\noindent\textit{Formal scope (editorial).} The counted material below is the exact formal relation-implication result in the fixed companion code. The paper uses finite-field language; the code states the A3 part over an arbitrary ring, the B3-small part over a field with nonzero 2, and the B3-large part additionally assumes that every element is a sum of two squares. The three parts form one collected result; no group-matrix identification or HDX corollary is added.
