\begin{theo} \label{th:main}
Let $ S$ be an oriented closed surface of genus $g\geq 2$. If $f\in \Homeo_*(S)$ preserves a Borel probability measure $\lambda$ such that $\suppp(\lambda)=S$ and $\rot_{f}(\lambda)\in \R H_1(S,\Z)$, then $f$ has infinitely many periodic points.

More precisely, for every ergodic measure $\nu\in\cM(f)$ that is not a Dirac measure at a contractible fixed point and every neighborhood $\cU$ of $\rot_{f}(\nu)$ in $H_1(S,\R)$, there exists a homotopical interval of rotation $(\kappa, r)$ such that $[\kappa]/r\in \cU$.
\end{theo}
