On the Elementary Construction of High-Dimensional Expanders by Kaufman and Oppenheim

Abstract: [Plain Text Version]

At STOC 2018, Kaufman and Oppenheim presented an elementary construction of high-dimensional spectral expanders using elementary matrices. We give a short, self-contained elementary proof of the correctness of their construction. As a bonus, this also yields a simple construction and analysis of standard expanders of bounded degree.
