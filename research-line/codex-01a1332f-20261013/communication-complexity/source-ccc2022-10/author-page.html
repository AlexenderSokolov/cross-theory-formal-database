I'm Guy, a Norbert Wiener Postdoctoral Fellow at MIT mentored by Ankur Moitra. Before that, I completed my PhD in the Stanford CS theory group where I was extremely fortunate to be advised by Li-Yang Tan.

My research is in theoretical computer science, with an emphasis on learning theory. I'm especially interested in developing theoretical frameworks that accurately capture properties that make learning algorithms effective in practice, such as robustness, privacy, and generalization. More generally, I'm drawn to broad and conceptual questions that are motivated by modern computational challenges. For example, my recent work adds to our understanding of the nature of problems that admit exponential quantum speedups.

My work has been recognized with a best paper award at SODA 2026, a best student paper award at STOC 2023, special issue invitations at STOC 2025, FOCS 2024, CCC 2024, and FOCS 2021, and a Jane Street Graduate Research Fellowship.
