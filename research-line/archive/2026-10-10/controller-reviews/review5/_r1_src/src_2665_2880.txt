2665| Since $\bar\Lambda$ is henselian and $F(\alpha^q)$ is contained
2666| in its residue field we can find a factorization
2667| $D' \to \bar \Lambda$. By the first part of the argument
2668| we see that $\bar\lambda^{qq'} \in D'$ for some $q' > 0$.
2669| \end{proof}

2671| \begin{lemma}
2672| \label{lemma-resolve-general}
2673| Let $k \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$ be as in
2674| Situation \ref{situation-local} where
2675| \begin{enumerate}
2676| \item $k$ is a field of characteristic $p > 0$,
2677| \item $\Lambda$ is Noetherian and geometrically regular over $k$,
2678| \item $\mathfrak q$ is minimal over $\mathfrak h_A$.
2679| \end{enumerate}
2680| Then $k \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$ can be resolved.
2681| \end{lemma}
2682| 
2683| \begin{proof}
2684| The lemma is proven by the following steps in the given order.
2685| We will justify each of these steps below.
2686| \begin{enumerate}
2687| \item
2688| \label{item-power}
2689| Pick an integer $N > 0$ such that
2690| $\mathfrak q^N\Lambda_\mathfrak q \subset H_{A/k}\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2691| \item
2692| \label{item-generators}
2693| Pick generators $a_1, \ldots, a_t \in A$ of the ideal $H_{A/R}$.
2694| \item
2695| \label{item-dimension}
2696| Set $d = \dim(\Lambda_\mathfrak q)$.
2697| \item
2698| \label{item-standardizer}
2699| Set $B = A[x_1, \ldots, x_d, z_{ij}]/(x_i^{2N} - \sum z_{ij}a_j)$.
2700| \item
2701| \label{item-elkik}
2702| Consider $B$ as a $k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]$-algebra and let
2703| $B \to C$ be as in
2704| Lemma \ref{lemma-improve-presentation}.
2705| We also obtain a section $C \to B$.
2706| \item
2707| \label{item-strictly-standard}
2708| Choose $c > 0$ such that each $x_i^c$
2709| is strictly standard in $C$ over $k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]$.
2710| \item
2711| \label{item-set-n}
2712| Set $n = N + dc$ and $e = 8c$.
2713| \item
2714| \label{item-set-E}
2715| Let $E \subset \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2716| be the images of generators of $A$ as a $k$-algebra.
2717| \item
2718| \label{item-NP}
2719| Choose an integer $m$ and a $k$-algebra map
2720| $\varphi : k[y_1, \ldots, y_m] \to \Lambda$
2721| and a factorization by local Artinian rings
2722| $$
2723| k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p
2724| \to D \to
2725| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2726| $$
2727| such that the first arrow is essentially smooth, the second is flat,
2728| $E$ is contained in $D$, with $\mathfrak p = \varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$
2729| the map $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is
2730| flat, and $\mathfrak p \Lambda_\mathfrak q = \mathfrak q \Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2731| \item
2732| \label{item-choose-pii}
2733| Choose $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_d \in \mathfrak p$ which map to a
2734| regular system of parameters of $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p$.
2735| \item
2736| \label{item-set-R}
2737| Let $R = k[y_1, \ldots, y_m, t_1, \ldots, t_m]$ and $\gamma_i = \pi_i t_i$.
2738| \item
2739| \label{item-modify-pii}
2740| If necessary modify the choice of $\pi_i$ such that
2741| for $i = 1, \ldots, d$ we have 
2742| $$
2743| \text{Ann}_{R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_{i - 1}^e)R}(\gamma_i)
2744| =
2745| \text{Ann}_{R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_{i - 1}^e)R}(\gamma_i^2)
2746| $$
2747| \item
2748| \label{item-choose-deltai}
2749| There exist $\delta_1, \ldots, \delta_d \in \Lambda$,
2750| $\delta_i \not \in \mathfrak q$ and a factorization
2751| $D \to D' \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2752| with $D'$ local Artinian, $D \to D'$ essentially smooth, the map
2753| $D' \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2754| flat such that, with $\pi_i' = \delta_i \pi_i$, we have for
2755| $i = 1, \ldots, d$
2756| \begin{enumerate}
2757| \item $(\pi_i')^{2N} = \sum a_j\lambda_{ij}$ in $\Lambda$ where
2758| $\lambda_{ij} \bmod \mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is an element of $D'$,
2759| \item $\text{Ann}_{\Lambda/({\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_{i - 1}^e)}({\pi'}_i) =
2760| \text{Ann}_{\Lambda/({\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_{i - 1}^e)}({\pi'}_i^2)$,
2761| \item $\delta_i \bmod \mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is an element of $D'$.
2762| \end{enumerate}
2763| \item
2764| \label{item-map-B-Lambda}
2765| Define $B \to \Lambda$ by sending $x_i$ to $\pi'_i$ and
2766| $z_{ij}$ to $\lambda_{ij}$ found above. Define $C \to \Lambda$
2767| by composing the map $B \to \Lambda$ with the retraction $C \to B$.
2768| \item
2769| \label{item-set-map-R}
2770| Map $R \to \Lambda$ by $\varphi$ on $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]$
2771| and by sending $t_i$ to $\delta_i$. Further introduce a map
2772| $$
2773| k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]
2774| \longrightarrow
2775| R = k[y_1, \ldots, y_m, t_1, \ldots, t_d]
2776| $$
2777| by sending $x_i$ to $\gamma_i = \pi_i t_i$.
2778| \item
2779| \label{item-first-solve}
2780| It suffices to resolve
2781| $$
2782| R
2783| \to
2784| C \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} R
2785| \to
2786| \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q
2787| $$
2788| \item
2789| \label{item-set-I}
2790| Set $I = (\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_d^e) \subset R$.
2791| \item
2792| \label{item-second-resolve}
2793| It suffices to resolve
2794| $$
2795| R/I
2796| \to
2797| C \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} R/I
2798| \to
2799| \Lambda/I\Lambda \supset \mathfrak q/I\Lambda
2800| $$
2801| \item
2802| \label{item-set-r}
2803| We denote $\mathfrak r \subset R = k[y_1, \ldots, y_m, t_1, \ldots, t_d]$
2804| the inverse image of $\mathfrak q$.
2805| \item
2806| \label{item-third-resolve}
2807| It suffices to resolve
2808| $$
2809| (R/I)_\mathfrak r \to
2810| C \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} (R/I)_\mathfrak r \to
2811| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/I\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2812| \supset
2813| \mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q/I\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2814| $$
2815| \item
2816| \label{item-fourth-resolve}
2817| Set $J = (\pi_1^e, \ldots, \pi_d^e)$ in $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]$.
2818| \item
2819| \label{item-fifth-resolve}
2820| It suffices to resolve
2821| $$
2822| (R/JR)_\mathfrak p \to
2823| C \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} (R/JR)_\mathfrak p \to
2824| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/J\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2825| \supset
2826| \mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q/J\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2827| $$
2828| \item
2829| \label{item-sixth-resolve}
2830| It suffices to resolve
2831| $$
2832| (R/\mathfrak p^nR)_\mathfrak p \to
2833| C \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} (R/\mathfrak p^nR)_\mathfrak p \to
2834| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2835| \supset
2836| \mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2837| $$
2838| \item
2839| \label{item-seventh-resolve}
2840| It suffices to resolve
2841| $$
2842| (R/\mathfrak p^nR)_\mathfrak p \to
2843| B \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} (R/\mathfrak p^nR)_\mathfrak p \to
2844| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2845| \supset
2846| \mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2847| $$
2848| \item
2849| \label{item-eighth-resolve}
2850| The ring $D'[t_1, \ldots, t_d]$ is given the structure of an
2851| $R_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^nR_\mathfrak p$-algebra by the given map
2852| $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p
2853| \to D'$ and by sending $t_i$ to $t_i$. It suffices to find a factorization
2854| $$
2855| B \otimes_{k[x_1, \ldots, x_d]} (R/\mathfrak p^nR)_\mathfrak p
2856| \to D'[t_1, \ldots, t_d] \to
2857| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2858| $$
2859| where the second arrow sends $t_i$ to $\delta_i$ and induces the given
2860| homomorphism $D' \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2861| \item
2862| \label{item-done}
2863| Such a factorization exists by our choice of $D'$ above.
2864| \end{enumerate}
2865| We now give the justification for each of the steps, except that we
2866| skip justifying the steps which just introduce notation.

2869| Ad (\ref{item-power}). This is possible as $\mathfrak q$ is minimal

2879| with finite free module of differentials. Hence some power of
2880| $x_i$ is strictly standard in $C$ over $k[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$
