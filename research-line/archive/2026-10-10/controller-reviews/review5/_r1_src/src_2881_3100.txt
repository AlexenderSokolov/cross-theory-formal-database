2881| by Lemma \ref{lemma-compare-standard}.

2887| Ad (\ref{item-choose-pii}). Since
2888| $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is
2889| flat and $\mathfrak p \Lambda_\mathfrak q = \mathfrak q \Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2890| by construction
2891| we see that $\dim(k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p) = d$ by
2892| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-dimension-base-fibre-equals-total}.
2893| Thus we can find $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_d \in \Lambda$ which map to
2894| a regular system of parameters in $\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.

2896| \medskip\noindent
2897| Ad (\ref{item-modify-pii}). By
2898| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-regular-ring-CM}
2899| any permutation of the sequence $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_d$ is a
2900| regular sequence in $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p$. Hence
2901| $\gamma_1 = \pi_1 t_1, \ldots, \gamma_d = \pi_d t_d$ is a regular
2902| sequence in
2903| $R_\mathfrak p = k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p[t_1, \ldots, t_d]$, see
2904| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-regular-sequence-in-polynomial-ring}.
2905| Let $S = k[y_1, \ldots, y_m] \setminus \mathfrak p$ so that
2906| $R_\mathfrak p = S^{-1}R$. Note that $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_d$
2907| and $\gamma_1, \ldots, \gamma_d$
2908| remain regular sequences if we multiply our $\pi_i$ by elements of $S$.
2909| Suppose that
2910| $$
2911| \text{Ann}_{R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_{i - 1}^e)R}(\gamma_i)
2912| =
2913| \text{Ann}_{R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_{i - 1}^e)R}(\gamma_i^2)
2914| $$
2915| holds for $i = 1, \ldots, t$ for some $t \in \{0, \ldots, d\}$. Note that
2916| $\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_t^e, \gamma_{t + 1}$ is a
2917| regular sequence in $S^{-1}R$ by
2918| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-regular-sequence-powers}.
2919| Hence we see that
2920| $$
2921| \text{Ann}_{S^{-1}R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_{i - 1}^e)}(\gamma_i) =
2922| \text{Ann}_{S^{-1}R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_{i - 1}^e)}(\gamma_i^2).
2923| $$
2924| Thus we get
2925| $$
2926| \text{Ann}_{R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_t^e)R}(\gamma_{t + 1})
2927| =
2928| \text{Ann}_{R/(\gamma_1^e, \ldots, \gamma_t^e)R}(\gamma_{t + 1}^2)
2929| $$
2930| after replacing $\pi_{t + 1}$ by $s\pi_{t + 1}$ for some $s \in S$ by
2931| Lemma \ref{lemma-ogoma}. By induction on $t$ this produces the desired
2932| sequence.

2935| Ad (\ref{item-choose-deltai}). Let $S = \Lambda \setminus \mathfrak q$
2936| so that $\Lambda_\mathfrak q = S^{-1}\Lambda$. Set
2937| $\bar \Lambda = \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n \Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2938| Suppose that we have a $t \in \{0, \ldots, d\}$ and
2939| $\delta_1, \ldots, \delta_t \in S$ and a factorization
2940| $D \to D' \to \bar \Lambda$ as in (\ref{item-choose-deltai})
2941| such that (a), (b), (c) hold for $i = 1, \ldots, t$. We have
2942| $\pi_{t + 1}^N \in H_{A/k}\Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2943| as $\mathfrak q^N \Lambda_\mathfrak q \subset H_{A/k}\Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2944| by (\ref{item-power}). Hence
2945| $\pi_{t + 1}^N \in H_{A/k} \bar\Lambda$. Hence
2946| $\pi_{t + 1}^N \in H_{A/k}D'$ as $D' \to \bar \Lambda$
2947| is faithfully flat, see
2948| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-faithfully-flat-universally-injective}.
2949| Recall that $H_{A/k} = (a_1, \ldots, a_t)$.
2950| Say $\pi_{t + 1}^N = \sum a_j d_j$ in $D'$ and choose
2951| $c_j \in \Lambda_\mathfrak q$ lifting $d_j \in D'$. Then
2952| $\pi_{t + 1}^N = \sum c_j a_j + \epsilon$ with
2953| $\epsilon \in \mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q \subset
2954| \mathfrak q^{n - N}H_{A/k}\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2955| Write $\epsilon = \sum a_j c'_j$ for some
2956| $c'_j \in \mathfrak q^{n - N}\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2957| Hence $\pi_{t + 1}^{2N} = \sum (\pi_{t + 1}^N c_j + \pi_{t + 1}^N c'_j) a_j$.
2958| Note that $\pi_{t + 1}^Nc'_j$ maps to zero in $\bar \Lambda$; this trivial
2959| but key observation will ensure later that (a) holds.
2960| Now we choose $s \in S$ such that there exist
2961| $\mu_{t + 1j} \in \Lambda$ such that on the one hand
2962| $\pi_{t + 1}^N c_j + \pi_{t + 1}^N c'_j = \mu_{t + 1j}/s^{2N}$
2963| in $S^{-1}\Lambda$ and on the other
2964| $(s \pi_{t + 1})^{2N} = \sum \mu_{t + 1j}a_j$
2965| in $\Lambda$ (minor detail omitted). We may further replace $s$ by
2966| a power and enlarge $D'$ such that $s$ maps to an element of $D'$.
2967| With these choices $\mu_{t + 1j}$ maps to $s^{2N}d_j$ which is
2968| an element of $D'$. Note that $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_d$ are a regular
2969| sequence of parameters in $S^{-1}\Lambda$ by our
2970| choice of $\varphi$. Hence $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_d$ forms a regular sequence
2971| in $\Lambda_\mathfrak q$ by
2972| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-regular-ring-CM}.
2973| It follows that ${\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_t^e, s\pi_{t + 1}$ is a
2974| regular sequence in $S^{-1}\Lambda$ by
2975| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-regular-sequence-powers}.
2976| Thus we get
2977| $$
2978| \text{Ann}_{S^{-1}\Lambda/({\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_t^e)}(s\pi_{t + 1}) =
2979| \text{Ann}_{S^{-1}\Lambda/({\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_t^e)}((s\pi_{t + 1})^2).
2980| $$
2981| Hence we may apply Lemma \ref{lemma-ogoma} to find an $s' \in S$
2982| such that
2983| $$
2984| \text{Ann}_{\Lambda/({\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_t^e)}((s')^qs\pi_{t + 1})
2985| =
2986| \text{Ann}_{\Lambda/({\pi'}_1^e, \ldots, {\pi'}_t^e)}(((s')^qs\pi_{t + 1})^2).
2987| $$
2988| for any $q > 0$. By Lemma \ref{lemma-enlarge-solution-modulo}
2989| we can choose $q$ and enlarge $D'$ such that $(s')^q$ maps to an element
2990| of $D'$. Setting $\delta_{t + 1} = (s')^qs$ and we conclude that
2991| (a), (b), (c) hold for $i = 1, \ldots, t + 1$. For (a) note that
2992| $\lambda_{t + 1j} = (s')^{2Nq}\mu_{t + 1j}$ works.
2993| By induction on $t$ we win.
