2545| algebras, as $K$ is the filtered union of the subfields $F$.
2546| Finally, these algebras are essentially of finite type over $k$ by
2547| Algebra, Lemma
2548| \ref{algebra-lemma-essentially-of-finite-type-into-artinian-local}.
2549| \end{proof}

2551| \begin{lemma}
2552| \label{lemma-solution-modulo}
2553| Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $p > 0$.
2554| Let $\Lambda$ be a Noetherian geometrically regular $k$-algebra.
2555| Let $\mathfrak q \subset \Lambda$ be a prime ideal.
2556| Let $n \geq 1$ be an integer and let
2557| $E \subset \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2558| be a finite subset.
2559| Then we can find $m \geq 0$ and
2560| $\varphi : k[y_1, \ldots, y_m] \to \Lambda$ with the following properties
2561| \begin{enumerate}
2562| \item setting $\mathfrak p = \varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ we have
2563| $\mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q = \mathfrak p \Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2564| and $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is flat,
2565| \item there is a factorization by homomorphisms of local Artinian rings
2566| $$
2567| k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p
2568| \to D \to
2569| \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q
2570| $$
2571| where the first arrow is essentially smooth and the second is flat,
2572| \item $E$ is contained in $D$ modulo $\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2573| \end{enumerate}
2574| \end{lemma}
2575| 
2576| \begin{proof}
2577| Set $\bar \Lambda = \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n\Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2578| Note that $\dim H_1(L_{\kappa(\mathfrak q)/k}) < \infty$ by
2579| More on Algebra, Proposition
2580| \ref{more-algebra-proposition-characterization-geometrically-regular}.
2581| Pick $A \subset \bar \Lambda$ containing $E$ such that $A$ is local
2582| Artinian, essentially of finite type over $k$, the map
2583| $A \to \bar \Lambda$ is flat, and $\mathfrak m_A$ generates the maximal
2584| ideal of $\bar \Lambda$, see Lemma \ref{lemma-helper}.
2585| Denote $F = A/\mathfrak m_A$ the residue field so that $k \subset F \subset K$.
2586| Pick $\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_t \in \Lambda$ which map
2587| to elements of $A$ in $\bar \Lambda$ such that moreover the images
2588| of $\text{d}\lambda_1, \ldots, \text{d}\lambda_t$ form a basis
2589| of $\Omega_{F/k}$. Consider the map
2590| $\varphi' : k[y_1, \ldots, y_t] \to \Lambda$ sending $y_j$ to $\lambda_j$.
2591| Set $\mathfrak p' = (\varphi')^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$. By
2592| More on Algebra, Lemma
2593| \ref{more-algebra-lemma-geometrically-regular-over-field}
2594| the ring map $k[y_1, \ldots, y_t]_{\mathfrak p'} \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q$
2595| is flat and $\Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p' \Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is
2596| regular. Thus we can choose further elements
2597| $\lambda_{t + 1}, \ldots, \lambda_m \in \Lambda$
2598| which map into $A \subset \bar \Lambda$ and which
2599| map to a regular system of parameters of
2600| $\Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p' \Lambda_\mathfrak q$.
2601| We obtain $\varphi : k[y_1, \ldots, y_m] \to \Lambda$ having
2602| property (1) such that
2603| $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p
2604| \to \bar\Lambda$
2605| factors through $A$. Thus
2606| $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p
2607| \to A$ is flat by
2608| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-flatness-descends-more-general}.
2609| By construction the residue field extension $F/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$
2610| is finitely generated and $\Omega_{F/\kappa(\mathfrak p)} = 0$. Hence it is
2611| finite separable by
2612| More on Algebra, Lemma \ref{more-algebra-lemma-cartier-equality}.
2613| Thus
2614| $k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p
2615| \to A$
2616| is finite by Algebra, Lemma
2617| \ref{algebra-lemma-essentially-of-finite-type-into-artinian-local}.
2618| Finally, we conclude that it is \'etale by
2619| Algebra, Lemma \ref{algebra-lemma-characterize-etale}.
2620| Since an \'etale ring map is certainly essentially smooth we win.
2621| \end{proof}

2623| \begin{lemma}
2624| \label{lemma-enlarge-solution-modulo}
2625| Let $\varphi : k[y_1, \ldots, y_m] \to \Lambda$, $n$, $\mathfrak q$,
2626| $\mathfrak p$ and
2627| $$
2628| k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n \to
2629| D \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n \Lambda_\mathfrak q
2630| $$
2631| be as in Lemma \ref{lemma-solution-modulo}. Then for any
2632| $\lambda \in \Lambda \setminus \mathfrak q$
2633| there exists an integer $q > 0$ and a factorization
2634| $$
2635| k[y_1, \ldots, y_m]_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak p^n \to
2636| D \to D' \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^n \Lambda_\mathfrak q
2637| $$
2638| such that $D \to D'$ is an essentially smooth map of local Artinian rings,
2639| the last arrow is flat, and $\lambda^q$ is in $D'$.
2640| \end{lemma}
