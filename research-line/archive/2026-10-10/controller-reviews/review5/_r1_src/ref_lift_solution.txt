2096| \begin{lemma}
2097| \label{lemma-lift-solution}
2098| Let $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$ be as in
2099| Situation \ref{situation-local}. Let $r \geq 1$ and
2100| $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_r \in R$ map to elements of $\mathfrak q$. Assume
2101| \begin{enumerate}
2102| \item for $i = 1, \ldots, r$ we have
2103| $$
2104| \text{Ann}_{R/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_{i - 1}^8)R}(\pi_i)
2105| =
2106| \text{Ann}_{R/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_{i - 1}^8)R}(\pi_i^2)
2107| $$
2108| and
2109| $$
2110| \text{Ann}_{\Lambda/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_{i - 1}^8)\Lambda}(\pi_i)
2111| =
2112| \text{Ann}_{\Lambda/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_{i - 1}^8)\Lambda}(\pi_i^2)
2113| $$
2114| \item for $i = 1, \ldots, r$ the element $\pi_i$ maps to a strictly
2115| standard element in $A$ over $R$.
2116| \end{enumerate}
2117| Then, if
2118| $$
2119| R/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_r^8)R \to A/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_r^8)A
2120| \to \Lambda/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_r^8)\Lambda \supset
2121| \mathfrak q/(\pi_1^8, \ldots, \pi_r^8)\Lambda
2122| $$
2123| can be resolved, so can $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$.
2124| \end{lemma}
2125| 
2126| \begin{proof}
2127| We are going to prove this by induction on $r$.
2128| 
2129| \medskip\noindent
2130| The case $r = 1$. Here the assumption is that there exists a
2131| factorization $A/\pi_1^8 \to \bar C \to \Lambda/\pi_1^8$
2132| which resolves the situation modulo $\pi_1^8$. Conditions (1) and
2133| (2) are the assumptions needed to apply
2134| Lemma \ref{lemma-desingularize-lifting-apply}.
