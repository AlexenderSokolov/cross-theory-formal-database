2060| By Lemma \ref{lemma-desingularize-strictly-standard}
2061| we can find $A \to B \to \Lambda$ with $B$ smooth over $R$
2062| which finishes the proof.
2063| \end{proof}

2078| \begin{situation}
2079| \label{situation-local}
2080| We are given a Noetherian ring $R$ and an $R$-algebra map $A \to \Lambda$
2081| and a prime $\mathfrak q \subset \Lambda$. We assume $A$ is of
2082| finite presentation over $R$. In this situation we denote
2083| $\mathfrak h_A = \sqrt{H_{A/R} \Lambda}$.
2084| \end{situation}
2085| 
2086| \noindent
2087| Let $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$ be as in
2088| Situation \ref{situation-local}. We say
2089| {\it $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$
2090| can be resolved} if there exists a factorization $A \to B \to \Lambda$
2091| with $B$ of finite presentation and
2092| $\mathfrak h_A \subset \mathfrak h_B \not \subset \mathfrak q$.

2094| a {\it resolution of $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$}.
