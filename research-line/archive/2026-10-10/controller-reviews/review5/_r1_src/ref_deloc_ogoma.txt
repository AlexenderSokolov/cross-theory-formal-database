2225| which is smooth over $R$ by construction.
2226| Since $b$ does not map to an element of $\mathfrak q$, we win.
2227| \end{proof}
2228| 
2229| \begin{lemma}
2230| \label{lemma-delocalize-height-zero}
2231| Let $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$ be as in
2232| Situation \ref{situation-local}. Let $\mathfrak p = R \cap \mathfrak q$.
2233| Assume
2234| \begin{enumerate}
2235| \item $\mathfrak q$ is minimal over $\mathfrak h_A$,
2236| \item $R_\mathfrak p \to A_\mathfrak p \to \Lambda_\mathfrak q
2237| \supset \mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q$ can be resolved, and
2238| \item $\dim(\Lambda_\mathfrak q) = 0$.
2239| \end{enumerate}
2240| Then $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$ can be resolved.
2241| \end{lemma}
2242| 
2243| \begin{proof}
2244| By (3) the ring $\Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is Artinian local hence
2245| $\mathfrak q\Lambda_\mathfrak q$ is nilpotent. Thus
2246| $(\mathfrak h_A)^N \Lambda_\mathfrak q = 0$ for some $N > 0$.
2247| Thus there exists a $\lambda \in \Lambda$, $\lambda \not \in \mathfrak q$
2248| such that $\lambda (\mathfrak h_A)^N = 0$ in $\Lambda$.
2249| Say $H_{A/R} = (a_1, \ldots, a_r)$ so that $\lambda a_i^N = 0$
2250| in $\Lambda$. By Lemma \ref{lemma-delocalize-weak} we can find a factorization
2251| $A \to C \to \Lambda$ with $C$ of finite presentation such that
2252| $\mathfrak h_C \not \subset \mathfrak q$.
2253| Write $C = A[x_1, \ldots, x_n]/(f_1, \ldots, f_m)$.
2254| Set
2255| $$
2256| B = A[x_1, \ldots, x_n, y_1, \ldots, y_r, z, t_{ij}]/
2257| (f_j - \sum y_i t_{ij}, zy_i)
2258| $$
2259| where $t_{ij}$ is a set of $rm$ variables.
2260| Note that there is a map $B \to C[y_i, z]/(y_iz)$ given by setting $t_{ij}$
2261| equal to zero. The map $B \to \Lambda$ is the composition
2262| $B \to C[y_i, z]/(y_iz) \to \Lambda$ where $C[y_i, z]/(y_iz) \to \Lambda$
2263| is the given map $C \to \Lambda$, maps $z$ to $\lambda$, and maps
2264| $y_i$ to the image of $a_i^N$ in $\Lambda$.
2265| 
2266| \medskip\noindent
2267| We claim that $B$ is a solution for $R \to A \to \Lambda \supset \mathfrak q$.
2268| First note that $B_z$ is isomorphic to $C[y_1, \ldots, y_r, z, z^{-1}]$
2269| and hence is smooth. On the other hand,
2270| $B_{y_\ell} \cong A[x_i, y_i, y_\ell^{-1}, t_{ij}, i \not = \ell]$
2271| which is smooth over $A$. Thus we see that $z$ and $a_\ell y_\ell$
2272| (compositions of smooth maps are smooth) are all
2273| elements of $H_{B/R}$. This proves the lemma.
2274| \end{proof}

2279| \section{Separable residue fields}
2280| \label{section-separable}
2281| 
2282| \noindent
2283| In this section we explain how to solve a local problem in the case
2284| of a separable residue field extension.
2285| 
2286| \begin{lemma}[Ogoma]
2287| \label{lemma-ogoma}
2288| Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring and let $M$ be a finite $A$-module.
2289| Let $S \subset A$ be a multiplicative set. If $\pi \in A$ and
2290| $\Ker(\pi : S^{-1}M \to S^{-1}M) =
2291| \Ker(\pi^2 : S^{-1}M \to S^{-1}M)$
2292| then there exists an $s \in S$ such that for any $n > 0$ we have
2293| $\Ker(s^n\pi : M \to M) = \Ker((s^n\pi)^2 : M \to M)$.
2294| \end{lemma}
2295| 
2296| \begin{proof}
2297| Let $K = \Ker(\pi : M \to M)$ and
2298| $K' = \{m \in M \mid \pi^2 m = 0\text{ in }S^{-1}M\}$ and
2299| $Q = K'/K$. Note that $S^{-1}Q = 0$ by assumption. Since $A$
2300| is Noetherian we see that $Q$ is a finite $A$-module.
