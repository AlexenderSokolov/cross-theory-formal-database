\begin{abstract}
    We study the average-case hardness of establishing that a graph does not have a large clique in both proof and communication complexity.
    We show exponential lower bounds on the length of cutting planes and 
    bounded-depth resolution over parities 
    refutations 
    of the binary encoding of clique formulas on randomly sampled dense graphs. 
    Moreover, we show that  
    the randomized communication complexity of 
    finding a falsified clause in these formulas 
    is polynomial.
\end{abstract}
